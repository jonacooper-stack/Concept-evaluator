# Round 2 — Seeded Idea Pool (idea-generator, Opus)

Seeded toward the refined winning shape distilled from the 4 councils: forced/recurring/
budgeted mandate; sleepy NON-TECH incumbents only (no Vanta/Drata/AgentSync/Vertafore/Clio/
Unit21/Sovos/OneTrust); light integration (doc/attestation/filing spine, no EMR/core-banking/
LIS/single-gated-rail); no uninsurable criminal tail (no perjury-grade/FCA/ITAR/classified);
no single scarce credentialed FTE as throughput ceiling; minimal PHI; recurring, low-capital,
remote, NOT field-ops.

Format: `<name> — <customer> | <forced/recurring must-have pain & why now> | <recurring revenue mechanism> | <sleepy NON-TECH incumbents & why no strong-tech player & founder edge>`
