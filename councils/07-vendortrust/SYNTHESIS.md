## ON HARD-CONSTRAINT #5
NOT a fail. The under-served supplier-side, multi-buyer, done-for-you concierge lane genuinely holds — buyer-side players (TrustLayer/myCOI/Jones) and HSE supplier-networks (Avetta/ISN) do not own it. A competitor to out-position, not a strong-tech incumbent.

## WHERE EXPERTS AGREE
- Pain real, forced, recurring, budgeted (lapse → PO blocked/payment withheld/deactivation = direct revenue loss).
- Archetype-clean shape: capital-light, fully remote, no field ops, no licensing cliff, revenue can precede software.
- One operational variable decides it (CFO/COO/CTO unanimous): per-account labor — ~45 min/month (software-leveraged, ~70% GM) vs 2–3 hours (staffing-heavy BPO). A 10-account time study must run before commit.
- Thin defensibility (scope/service moat) + portal dependency (operating inside portals the founders don't own).

## WHERE EXPERTS DISAGREE (PM call)
- GO (CFO/COO/Legal) vs REFINE (CMO/CTO) → **CMO/CTO frame load-bearing**: market-pull is the heaviest dimension (wt 20), and the CMO is the only seat that interrogated felt-urgency, scoring it 5 ("the difference between a 9 and a 4"). Can't GO on operational cleanliness alone when WTP is unproven.
- CFO "comfortable $300K rung" vs CMO "diffuse customer, no dense channel" → **CMO governs**: the ladder math is a denominator problem; the numerator (acquirable accounts at acceptable CAC) is the real constraint.
- CTO "build is a clear GO" vs CTO's own REFINE → confirms the risk is commercial/operational, not technical; "mistaking a tracking dashboard for the elimination of labor."

## TOP 5 RISKS (ranked)
1. Willingness-to-pay in this tier unproven; no one MANDATES the service (Avetta/ISN work because the buyer mandates the network; here the supplier must volunteer). Risk of a vitamin wearing a painkiller's coat.
2. Per-account labor is a BPO in disguise (45 min vs 2–3 hours).
3. Diffuse customer with no dense acquisition channel.
4. Thin defensibility / fast-follow risk (an Avetta-adjacent network or SIM/TPRM platform could copy scope in <1 yr).
5. Professional E&O / portal-ToS exposure (missed deadline → quantifiable claim; stored reusable portal passwords a latent catastrophe).

## TOP 3 STRENGTHS
- Forced/recurring/budgeted pain with a hard monetary consequence; supplier-pays model proven at scale by Avetta/ISN/Veriforce; structurally suppressed churn.
- Archetype-clean operating + financial shape (remote, no field ops, no licensing cliff, positive cash cycle, 6–9-week MVP, revenue precedes software).
- A genuinely empty competitive lane that fits the founders' marketing/partner/founder-led edge; the buyer-requirement library is an accumulating ownable asset.

## BIGGEST OPEN QUESTION
When a large buyer threatens a hold, does the SMB supplier reach for a $200–$1,000/mo managed service, or does the office manager scramble and fix it free that week? Cheap pre-build test: interview 15+ suppliers who've sold into a large-buyer portal — how many were held/withheld in the last 12 months, and how many say *unprompted* they'd pay to make it go away.
