- CTO build-ease (8s) vs business-risk (4s) → **the 4s govern**: "the software is the easy 10%"; the product is a perishable data corpus + human labor the cheap build can't touch.

## TOP 5 RISKS (ranked)
1. Minutes-per-filing turns this into a BPO, not software (margin 35% vs 15%; FTEs 3.8 vs 6+; violates the no-sustained-manual-work constraint).
2. Third-party filing barred for liquor/health collapses the value prop where WTP is highest.
3. Income ladder falls short (~$280K–$350K/founder by mo24; mo12 rung late, mo24 $500K rung missed).
4. Perishable rules-corpus maintenance treadmill (no national API; thousands of authorities; staleness defect rate in a near-zero-error product).
5. Reactive buyer defeats the cheap acquisition channel; low ACV means single-unit operators may never clear CAC.

## TOP 3 STRENGTHS
- A true painkiller with structural low churn and real WTP.
- Capital-light, fully remote, no field ops, clean legal posture (founders carry no credential).
- Genuine competitive air — no funded incumbent owns the 3–50-unit food-service operational-permit stack (Avalara/Harbor/CSC are horizontal-enterprise).
