## BIGGEST OPEN QUESTION
Does the EFAST2 late-filer outbound wedge actually puncture buyer self-perception denial at acceptable CAC and velocity? If "your 2023 5500 was filed 40 days late — here's your exposure" reliably converts at a 12-month payback and ~15–20 net adds/month, market pull rerates 5→7 and this becomes a GO. If the late-filer who survived without consequence shrugs, it stays a vitamin-in-perception and is a REFINE-or-drop. One 50–100-contact test collapses the largest uncertainty in the packet — run it before any build.

## PATH (validate before build; structural change or abandon if weak)
