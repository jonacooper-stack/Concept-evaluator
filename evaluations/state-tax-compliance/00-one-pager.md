# Concept One-Pager (clean room)

## The customer
US-incorporated startups and small-to-mid-sized businesses (roughly 10–250
employees) that operate across multiple states — typically venture-backed or
bootstrapped companies with remote employees, a parent C-corp plus one or more
subsidiaries, and revenue collected from customers in many states. The economic
buyer is the founder/CEO, the controller, or a fractional/full-time finance lead.
These companies do not have a dedicated multi-state tax department; compliance is
handled part-time by a generalist who is unsure whether they are actually
compliant.

## The pain
A company operating in many states accumulates a sprawling, hard-to-see set of
recurring government obligations:
- Entity-level taxes and reports: Delaware franchise tax, other states' franchise
  taxes, annual reports, registered-agent renewals — for the parent and every
  subsidiary.
- Employment-driven obligations: when an employee lives or works in a state, the
  company must register for payroll withholding and unemployment insurance, and
  keep filing there.
- Revenue/sales-driven obligations: economic nexus thresholds trigger sales-tax
  registration and recurring filing in states where the company sells.
- Foreign-qualification ("doing business") registrations in each state of
  operation.

Each obligation has its own forms, deadlines, payment cadence, and penalties for
missing them. Most small companies "blindly follow and hope it's right." They
lack a single place to see what applies to them, what is coming due, and whether
they are current. The fear of surprise penalties, back taxes, and being out of
good standing is constant and budgeted-for (they already pay accountants,
registered agents, and filing fees).

## The product
A web dashboard that gives a company a single, real-time compliance picture
across all states and all entities:
- An inventory of every obligation that applies to this specific company,
  derived from where its employees are, where its revenue comes from, where its
  entities are registered, and its corporate structure.
- A red / yellow / green status light for each obligation (compliant / due soon /
  overdue or unregistered).
- A calendar of what is due when, what is payable, amounts, and the filing
  destination.
- Guided workflows and plain-language explanations of each requirement, with the
  ability to file or hand off filing.
- Alerts when a new obligation is triggered (e.g., a new employee in a new state,
  or crossing a sales-tax nexus threshold).

## The revenue model
Annual SaaS subscription, tiered by number of entities, number of states, and
employee count. Indicative pricing in the low-to-mid four figures per year for a
small company, rising into five figures for multi-entity mid-market companies.
Optional done-for-you filing handled as a recurring add-on or per-filing fee.
Recurring by nature: obligations renew every year, so the product is used
continuously, not once.

## The positioning
"See and stay on top of every state tax and filing obligation your company has —
across every state and every entity — without a dedicated tax department." Sold
to finance leaders and founders who currently stitch together spreadsheets, their
accountant, a registered-agent portal, and guesswork. Acquisition is
content/positioning-led (the topic is confusing and widely searched) plus
founder-led sales into startup and finance communities.
