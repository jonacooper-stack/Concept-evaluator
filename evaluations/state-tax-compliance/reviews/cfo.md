CFO REVIEW — Multi-State Tax & Filing Compliance Dashboard

ONE-PARAGRAPH FINANCIAL READ
The financials here are genuinely attractive on the model side and genuinely scary on the delivery side. This is annual-prepaid SaaS into a budgeted, recurring, must-have compliance pain — 80%+ gross margins, negative working capital (cash in before costs), and self-funding growth. The number that does NOT trivially pencil is the income ladder: at the implied ~$3K-$8K ACV, reaching ~$300K/founder by month 12 needs roughly 100-160 paying customers in year one. The deeper financial risk is hidden: keeping the per-state, per-entity obligation rules engine accurate across ~50 jurisdictions is a recurring content-maintenance cost that looks like COGS, not a one-time build, and getting it wrong creates penalty-liability exposure.

TOP 3 STRENGTHS
- Negative working capital + annual-prepaid billing: growth self-funds, no raise required.
- High durable gross margin on a budgeted, renew-every-year compliance buy with structurally high retention.
- Excellent capital efficiency + exit optionality with real acquirer comps.

TOP 3 RISKS
- Hidden recurring COGS: maintaining a 50-state × multi-obligation rules engine quietly compresses GM and consumes founder time.
- Conversion uncertainty on a fuzzy trigger: buyer often doesn't KNOW they're non-compliant, inflating CAC.
- Accuracy/liability exposure: a wrong "green" → refunds, churn, indemnity.

BIGGEST SINGLE RISK
The recurring cost and liability of keeping the rules engine correct. The value prop is "trust our light instead of your guesswork," only as good as a continuously-updated, jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction ruleset across ~50 states and every entity. Rules change yearly; a wrong green is real money (penalties, back taxes, lost good standing). Financially this (1) converts ~85% margin to ~70-75% via ongoing research labor and (2) creates tail liability. If founders absorb with their own time it caps the business at their bandwidth and breaks the self-funding story; if they license content they hand margin/differentiation to a vendor that could become a competitor.

QUESTIONS THE FOUNDERS MUST ANSWER
- All-in annual cost to keep the rules engine accurate, and how it scales per state/entity? Show the COGS line.
- Realistic founder-led conversion rate and cycle for a buyer who doesn't yet know they're non-compliant; discovery calls per closed logo; blended ACV actually closed.
- Liability posture if a customer relies on a "green" and is penalized — indemnity, disclaimer, E&O cost — priced into margin?

RECOMMENDATION: GO (conditioned on proving rules-maintenance stays inside 70%+ effective GM and founder-led conversion clears ~11-15 logos/month).
