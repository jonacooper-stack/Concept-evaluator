IDEA: Member Engagement & Dues-Collection Overlay for Labor Unions
ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION: A software overlay on a union's legacy database that captures missing member contact data and runs a direct-to-member card/ACH dues-collection rail (bypassing employer payroll deduction), monetized via an SMS markup + a payment take-rate on dues collected.
