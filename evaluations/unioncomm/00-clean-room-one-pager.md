# Clean-Room One-Pager — Member Engagement & Dues-Collection Overlay for Labor Unions

## The customer
Labor unions in the United States — national bodies, state federations, districts/councils,
and local chapters — particularly those operating in states with restrictive labor
legislation (e.g., "right-to-work" and paycheck-protection regimes) where a union's ability
to collect dues through employer payroll deduction has been curtailed or eliminated. The
buyer is union leadership at the state/national level (executive directors, organizing
directors, membership/secretary-treasurer roles) who can mandate adoption down to locals.

## The pain
When dues can no longer be deducted automatically through the employer's payroll system,
a union must collect dues directly from each individual member. To do that it needs accurate
personal contact data (cell phone, personal email) for every member — data that is frequently
missing, stale, or trapped behind the employer relationship in a legacy database. Without a
direct-to-member contact channel and a direct payment rail, the union loses dues revenue,
loses members to lapse, and loses the ability to mobilize. This is an existential,
budget-threatening problem, not a convenience issue.

## The product
A lightweight software overlay that sits on top of a union's existing legacy database of
record (it integrates rather than replaces it). It provides:
- Multi-tiered, hierarchical communication channels mapped to the union's structure
  (national → state → district → local), over free email and SMS text.
- High-conversion digital onboarding/renewal workflows (structured digital renewal links)
  designed to capture missing member contact data — personal cell numbers and emails.
- A direct-to-member digital dues-collection rail (credit card and ACH direct debit) that
  operates independently of employer payroll deduction.
- Rapid, low-friction deployment across many locals without disrupting the underlying
  institutional database.

## Positioning
Against entrenched legacy Membership Management Systems and databases of record (notably
Union Link and UnionWare), which are heavy, desktop-first systems built for compliance and
grievance tracking and treat real-time digital communication and member engagement as
secondary. Those incumbents sell on long cycles with seat-licensed pricing. This product is
positioned as the modern, fast-to-deploy communication-and-payments layer that the legacy
systems don't provide — not a rip-and-replace.

## Revenue model
A freemium entry hook removes procurement and budget friction so that state/national bodies
can mandate and roll the software out to locals quickly. Monetization is usage- and
transaction-based rather than flat SaaS licensing:
- A limited markup on SMS text message volume.
- A payment-processing take-rate on all digital dues collected via card or ACH.
Costs and revenue therefore scale with member volume and usage. The model is designed to be
sticky (it becomes the dues rail) and to extend horizontally to adjacent membership
organizations — advocacy groups, professional associations, and non-profits.
