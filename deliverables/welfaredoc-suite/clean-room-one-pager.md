# Clean-Room Concept One-Pager
**Internal label:** Welfare-Plan Compliance Documents & Testing Service

> This is the neutral, standalone one-pager handed to each council reviewer. It contains no scores, no lineage, no tier bar, and no target — by design.

## 1) CUSTOMER
U.S. employers that sponsor group health & welfare benefit plans — primary focus on organizations of roughly 50–500 employees (large enough to owe the full slate of obligations but typically without in-house ERISA counsel). Reached predominantly through their employee-benefits brokers/agencies and benefits third-party administrators (TPAs), who serve as the distribution channel and would resell or white-label the service across their book of employer clients. A secondary direct-to-employer channel exists for HR/finance leaders (CFO, controller, HR director) who own benefits compliance.

## 2) THE MUST-HAVE / FORCING FUNCTION
Federal law (ERISA and the Internal Revenue Code) imposes mandatory, recurring documentation and testing obligations on employers that sponsor welfare benefit plans, each with hard deadlines and monetary penalties:
- **Written plan documents & Summary Plan Description (SPD):** ERISA requires a written plan document and an SPD furnished to participants. Insurance certificates alone do not satisfy this; a consolidating "wrap" document is the standard remedy. Failure to furnish an SPD/plan document upon DOL request carries a per-day penalty (~$190/day, capped per request), and absence of compliant documents increases fiduciary exposure in audits, disputes, and claims.
- **Annual Form 5500 (with Schedule A/C):** Welfare plans meeting the filing threshold (generally 100+ participants at plan-year start, plus funded plans) must file Form 5500 within 7 months of plan year-end. DOL late/non-filing penalties exceed $2,000 per day, indexed annually, with no statutory cap (a voluntary correction program reduces but does not eliminate exposure).
- **Annual nondiscrimination testing:** Plans receiving favorable tax treatment must pass annual tests — §125 cafeteria-plan testing, §105(h) self-insured health-plan testing, §129 dependent-care assistance testing, and §79 group-term-life testing. Failure makes the affected benefit taxable to highly-compensated/key employees and creates payroll-tax correction obligations.
- **§125 Premium-Only-Plan (POP) document:** A written §125 plan document is legally required for employees to pay benefit premiums pre-tax through payroll. Without it, pre-tax treatment is not allowed.

These obligations are not discretionary and the cost of non-compliance is concrete (daily-accruing penalties, loss of tax-qualified status, fiduciary/personal liability, adverse audit findings). They recur every plan year, and documents must be refreshed whenever plan designs, carriers, or laws change.

## 3) PRODUCT
A software-plus-service platform that: (a) generates and maintains the wrap SPD/plan document and the §125 POP document from a guided, plain-language intake; (b) prepares and (where authorized) electronically files the welfare Form 5500 and schedules; (c) runs the annual §125/§105(h)/§129/§79 nondiscrimination tests and outputs dated, audit-ready results plus correction guidance on failure; (d) maintains a per-plan compliance calendar with deadline tracking and renewal/refresh reminders each plan year; (e) stores executed documents, filings, and proof-of-distribution in an audit-ready vault. Delivered through a broker/TPA-facing portal that lets an agency administer many employer clients from one console, with employer-facing access for document distribution and e-signature.

## 4) REVENUE MODEL
Annual recurring subscription, priced per employer plan and tiered by employee count and modules (documents only vs. documents + 5500 + testing). Sold primarily through the benefits-broker/TPA channel as a white-label or referral arrangement (broker resells at a markup or embeds as a client value-add); a direct-to-employer plan is also offered. Revenue renews annually because the SPD/wrap must be kept current, the Form 5500 re-filed, and the nondiscrimination tests re-run every plan year.

## 5) POSITIONING
A modern, welfare-focused compliance platform purpose-built for the employee-benefits broker channel: fast guided document generation, plain-language compliance, and an annual deadline calendar that keeps employers and their brokers ahead of DOL and IRS obligations — consolidating documents, 5500 filing, and nondiscrimination testing for health & welfare plans in one place.
