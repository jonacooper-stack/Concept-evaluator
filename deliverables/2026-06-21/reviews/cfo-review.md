CFO REVIEW — Managed DFARS/CMMC Compliance-as-a-Service for Small Defense Subcontractors ("Artifact-Only")

ONE-PARAGRAPH FINANCIAL READ
The numbers pencil unusually well for a bootstrap: this is a high-margin, recurring, software-leveraged service ($18K–$42K ACV plus a ~$9.5K–$15K onboarding fee) sold into a forced, deadline-driven buy with an already-budgeted line item. Onboarding is paid upfront and roughly cash-funds delivery, so growth largely self-funds, CAC is low (SEO/free-assessment funnel + channel partners), and gross margin after a fractional credentialed reviewer realistically sits in the 65–80% band as the software absorbs the repeatable work. The two real financial fragilities are (1) the founder-income ladder depends on getting onboarding-heavy throughput up fast against a labor-bound expert review step, and (2) the entire demand thesis is timed to the CMMC Nov-10-2026 phase-in — a slip or softening of that enforcement compresses the urgency premium and lengthens sales cycles. Net: the financial model is genuinely strong; the risk is operational throughput and policy timing, not the unit economics.

- Throughput/labor ceiling on the expert layer: if NIST 800-171 deliverables need more senior human hours per account (or RP/CCP talent is scarce/expensive), COGS rises toward services levels (45–55%) and the income ladder needs more logos.
- Channel/CAC fragility and an attestation/liability tail: demand rides one organic channel; if it underperforms, paid CAC could climb. Separately, an "artifact-only" preparer whose package precedes a failed assessment or FCA exposure could face indemnity demands and rising E&O cost.

BIGGEST SINGLE RISK
The single financial issue most likely to bend this model is regulatory-timing dependence on the CMMC Level 2 phase-in. Every premium in the pricing — the 3–5× markup over DIY software, the willingness to pay $18K–$42K/yr plus a five-figure onboarding fee, and crucially the short, low-CAC sales cycle that makes the unit economics so attractive — rests on a present-tense, fast, concrete consequence: a blank/stale SPRS score or an unmet CMMC requirement costing the sub new or renewed purchase orders. DFARS 7012/7019/7020 and SPRS scoring are live today, which de-risks this meaningfully versus a pure CMMC bet, but the step-change in urgency is timed to Nov 10, 2026 and to primes actually enforcing flow-downs. If that phase-in slips again (the program has a history of timeline movement), if enforcement is lax, or if primes don't gate POs in practice, the forcing function weakens from "must buy now" toward "should get to eventually" — sales cycles lengthen, the premium compresses, and the customer count needed for the income ladder must be acquired against softer urgency and likely higher CAC. The mitigant is that the DFARS/SPRS obligation is already mandatory and already costing subs work, so even a delayed CMMC leaves a real, present, budgeted pain — but the founders should model a "phase-in slips 6–12 months" scenario explicitly.

QUESTIONS THE FOUNDERS MUST ANSWER BEFORE I'M COMFORTABLE
- What is the true fully-loaded reviewer cost per account? Time-box one mock onboarding and one quarterly cycle and measure credentialed-practitioner hours (and dollar cost) per customer per tier — this determines whether gross margin is 75% or 50%.
- What is the real customer tier mix and onboarding throughput ceiling? From the SPRS/DIB population and the first 15–20 free-assessment intakes, what fraction are <50 vs 50–120 vs 120–200 employees, and how many 4–8-week onboardings can the two of you plus a fractional reviewer run in parallel?
- What does the free-assessment funnel actually convert at, and what is the resulting CAC? Stand up the assessment + SEO/ads for 60–90 days and measure completions → consults → closed deals and cost per acquired customer.
- Will customers prepay quarterly/annually, and will you carry E&O/indemnity exposure as an artifact-only preparer? Prepay converts a strong working-capital profile into a self-financing engine; insurance/indemnity is a real cost line and a gating diligence item given FCA sensitivity.
