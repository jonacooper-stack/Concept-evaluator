# Clean-Room One-Pager (council input) — Self-Serve CMMC SaaS variant

> Neutral standalone restatement handed to each of the six council subagents for the
> SECOND concept (a CMMC-only self-serve software product). No brand name, no prior
> scores, no tier, no lineage disclosed (Fresh-Evaluation Protocol).

THE CUSTOMER. Small U.S. defense subcontractors — manufacturers and suppliers with
roughly 15–200 employees (e.g., a ~35-person precision machine shop) that handle
Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) on defense purchase orders. They typically
have one outsourced IT provider (MSP), no in-house security or compliance staff, and
the owner personally "owns" the compliance obligation. They are cost-sensitive and not
technical.

THE FORCING FUNCTION. U.S. defense rules (DFARS 252.204-7012/7019/7020) already require
any subcontractor that touches CUI to maintain a current cybersecurity self-assessment
score in the government's SPRS system, keep a live System Security Plan (SSP) and Plan
of Action & Milestones (POA&M), and answer primes' flow-down security questionnaires; a
blank/stale SPRS score can make a sub ineligible for new purchase orders now. CMMC Level
2 — a third-party assessment against the 110 NIST 800-171 controls — begins phasing into
new CUI contracts on Nov 10, 2026. The obligation is recurring (maintained every
quarter, indefinitely), already budgeted, and bought to avoid a fast, concrete
consequence: losing work with their primes.

THE PRODUCT. A self-serve, CMMC-specialized software product (SaaS) that lets the
subcontractor get and stay CMMC/NIST 800-171 ready themselves, fast and without a
consultant. The customer signs up online (no sales call), and the software guides them:
upload existing security policies → automated gap analysis against all 110 control
objectives → a deterministic estimated SPRS score (current and target) → auto-generated
SSP and POA&M documents → a prioritized, plain-English remediation plan → a reusable
library that auto-answers prime security questionnaires → continuous monitoring with
quarterly re-scoring, evidence-refresh reminders, and annual-affirmation prompts. The
product is specialized ONLY in CMMC / NIST 800-171 / SPRS (not a multi-framework GRC
tool). It is designed around speed (results in minutes, not months), low cost, and ease
of use for a non-technical owner. By design the system holds only compliance
documentation (policies, control status, SSP/POA&M text, questionnaire answers) — NOT
the customer's CUI or operational security data (configs, logs, scans) — so neither
party's sensitive data enters the tool. The customer does the work and self-attests; the
software provides estimates and generated drafts clearly labeled as such (it is not an
accredited assessor, provides no certification, and the customer is responsible for what
they post and attest to the government).

REVENUE MODEL. Low-cost, published, self-serve recurring subscription (credit-card
signup, no/low onboarding fee), tiered by company size/scope — roughly $200–$600/month
(~$2,400–$7,200 per year). Positioned as far cheaper than a consultant or a managed
service, and as a CMMC-only specialist alternative to broad GRC platforms. Optional
higher-touch "done-for-you" services may be layered on later as the company grows, but
the core business is the self-serve product.

POSITIONING / GO-TO-MARKET. Sold against: broad GRC automation platforms (e.g., Vanta,
Drata) that cover many frameworks but are complex and leave the work to the customer;
CMMC-specialized GRC tools (e.g., Totem, FutureFeed); managed-environment/enclave
providers and hourly consultants (expensive). The wedge is "the fastest, simplest,
CMMC-only way to get and stay ready — self-serve, low cost, no consultant, no dashboard
you can't run." Acquisition is product-led and marketing-led: a free automated gap
analysis as the top-of-funnel hook, SEO/content on present-tense SPRS/CMMC pain, and
channel partners (e.g., APEX Accelerators, manufacturing-extension centers). No
founder-led sales close is required to transact; the product is bought self-serve.

THE TEAM. Two founders who are strong at marketing/positioning and can build software,
with hands-on compliance-operator experience running SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA/HITECH, and
GDPR/CCPA programs. They do NOT hold a CMMC credential (e.g., Registered Practitioner /
Certified CMMC Professional) and deliberately plan to launch the self-serve product
WITHOUT hiring credentialed staff — a goal is to generate meaningful recurring revenue
from software without adding headcount, bringing in specialized experts only later as the
business grows. A senior DoD/DIB advisor informs the methodology and content.
