funeral homes, so a two-person office can stay current with price-disclosure rules
without a web agency or a lawyer.

- CAC [ASSUMED]: $1,000–$1,400 (outbound + event + association sponsorship). Payback = $1,200/($549×0.88) ≈ **2.5 months**. LTV (5-yr life × $6,588 × 0.88) ≈ $29k → LTV/CAC ≈ **24×** (conservative even at 3-yr life ≈ 14×).

## LEGAL / REGULATORY REVIEW — Everwell

(Not formal legal advice — operator pattern-recognition; retain counsel on the items below.)

ONE-PARAGRAPH RISK READ
Posture is good with two things to watch. The founders are a software vendor, not
a funeral provider, so they hold no funeral license and carry no funeral-board
licensure burden. The watch items are (a) liability if a compliance feature
produces a wrong disclosure, managed with disclaimers + E&O insurance, and (b)
keeping the templates current with the federal Funeral Rule and 50-state board
variation.

REGULATORY MAP
- **FTC Funeral Rule (16 CFR 453)** — governs price disclosure by funeral
  providers; the home is the regulated party, the software helps it comply. The
  Rule's modernization (toward online price posting) is a demand tailwind. Burden:
  content-maintenance, not licensure.
- **State funeral/cemetery boards** — vary by state; again regulate the provider,
  not the vendor.
- **State auto-renewal/ROSCA** — applies to the SaaS subscription; clear
  disclosure + easy cancel; low burden if built in from day one.
- **Standard data privacy (CCPA/CPRA et al.)** — decedent/next-of-kin PII; not
  HIPAA (deathcare generally falls outside HIPAA); manageable.

LICENSING SUMMARY
None for the software business beyond an ordinary business license. The funeral
home holds its own funeral-director license; the vendor does not touch it.

LIABILITY POSTURE
Primary exposure: a customer relies on a generated price list that is wrong and is
cited by a regulator. Mitigate with clear "vendor is not your compliance/legal
advisor" terms, a human-review step, and Tech E&O insurance (modest premium for a
SaaS at this scale). Entity (LLC/C-corp) shields personal exposure post-formation.
