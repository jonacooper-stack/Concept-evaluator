# Industry One-Pager (Stage-1 Industry Screen)

## The industry / business type
**Industrial water-treatment service** — independent companies that provide recurring
**chemical treatment and testing** for boilers, cooling towers, and closed-loop HVAC/process
water systems at commercial and industrial facilities (hospitals, universities, manufacturers,
large buildings, food/bev plants). The model is a **recurring service contract**: a service
technician visits on a schedule, tests the water, doses/delivers treatment chemicals, and
files compliance/performance reports. Revenue is the recurring service-contract fee plus
chemical sales, with occasional equipment/remediation work.

## Why this is a buy-and-modernize target type
Many of these firms were founded by water-treatment chemists/engineers now retiring, with very
sticky multi-year accounts and no modern systems or marketing. The service is **essential**
(prevents corrosion, scale, Legionella, and downtime of expensive equipment), regulated-
adjacent (Legionella/ASHRAE 188, environmental discharge), and the technical knowledge is a
**bridgeable moat** held by licensed/trained employees. Big players (e.g., Veolia/Nalco,
ChemTreat) chase large national accounts, leaving mid-market and regional accounts to sleepy
independents.

## Revenue mechanism (why it's durable)
Multi-year recurring service contracts (very sticky — switching risks equipment damage and
compliance lapses) plus recurring consumable chemical sales. High retention by nature.

## Operating model (typical)
Route-based service technicians (trained/licensed in water chemistry), a lab/reporting
function, and an owner who handles key technical accounts and sales. Field work done by the
crew; owner-operator manages. Industry constraint: technical talent and accurate reporting.

## The "sleepy" picture (modernization upside across the industry)
Typical operators run **manual lab logs, paper service reports, no customer portal or
dashboards, no CRM, no digital marketing**, and rarely raise prices. Modern test-reporting
dashboards, IoT/automated water monitoring, a customer compliance portal, contract-renewal
automation, and B2B marketing are broadly open. The founders' compliance-operator background
fits the reporting/regulatory side directly.

## Competitive structure
Fragmented regional independents below the national chemical majors' account threshold; not an
active SBA-tier PE bidding war.
