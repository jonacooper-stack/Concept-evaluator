# Industry One-Pager (Stage-1 Industry Screen)

## The industry / business type
**Niche industrial distribution — fasteners / industrial supply with vendor-managed inventory
(VMI)** — independent distributors that supply fasteners, fittings, MRO consumables, and
related industrial parts to manufacturers, fabricators, and contractors, often running **VMI**
(the distributor stocks, counts, and replenishes bins on the customer's production floor).
Revenue is recurring **repeat reorders** from a sticky base of B2B accounts, with the VMI
integration creating switching friction. No field trade crew, no trade license.

## Why this is a buy-and-modernize target type
A large, fragmented population of regional distributors are owned by retiring operators who
still take orders by **phone and fax**, have no e-commerce, no reorder automation, and no
pricing analytics. The business is **essential and embedded** in customers' production
(running out of a fastener stops a line), and national MRO platforms (Grainger, Fastenal,
Bunzl) pursue larger accounts, leaving sub-$5M regional distributors sleepy and un-modernized.
This is the **purest software-modernization gap** in the candidate set and needs no field
crew.

## Revenue mechanism (why it's durable)
Recurring repeat reorders from embedded B2B accounts; VMI integration into customers'
production lines creates real switching costs and reorder stickiness.

## Operating model (typical)
Inside sales/order desk, warehouse/pickers, delivery drivers or VMI route reps, and an owner
running key accounts and purchasing. No trade license; founders can run it as managers. The
binding economics are inventory turns, pricing discipline, and fill rate.

## The "sleepy" picture (modernization upside across the industry)
Typical operators run **phone/fax ordering, paper catalogs, a legacy/no ERP, no online
ordering, no reorder automation, no data-driven pricing, no digital marketing**. An e-commerce
reorder portal, automated/VMI replenishment, dynamic pricing analytics, and B2B marketing are
broadly open and high-ROI — the clearest pure-software lever in the set.

## Competitive structure
Fragmented regional distributors below the national MRO platforms' account threshold; not an
active SBA-tier PE bidding war.
