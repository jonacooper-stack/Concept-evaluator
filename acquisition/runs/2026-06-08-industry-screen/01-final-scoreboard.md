## Honest read
No industry cleared the strict numeric PURSUE floor (mean ≥8.0, no sub-score <7, objectives
≥80) — the honest field sits at **mean ~7.2–7.4**. That is a real, useful result: these are
**good (7 = good) industries to hunt in**, not rare 9s, and each carries one genuine
INDUSTRY-LEVEL drag that diligence must respect (it is not a company-specific unknown). The
top four are recommended PURSUE on judgment with that drag named; #5 is a MAYBE confined to a
sub-segment. Scores rose vs the earlier company-level run (which clustered ~6.0–6.6) precisely
because the universal company-specific drags were correctly moved to Stage 2.

## THE 3 INDUSTRIES TO HUNT IN (PURSUE) — with the brief for each

### 1. Commercial Fire & Life-Safety Inspection (mean 7.4) — strongest
- **Why:** demand is **mandated by fire code and re-occurs on a deadline** (the most durable
  recurring revenue in the set); asset-light (best DSCR — base ≈1.7x); a **hyper-local
  licensing moat** keeps national consolidators out of the small tier.
- **Target sub-segment / size band:** paper-based independents with **≥50% inspection
  revenue, ~$300K–$800K SDE**, in metros without heavy national-consolidator density
  (avoid markets Pye-Barker / Summit are actively rolling up).
- **Green flags on a real company:** high inspection-to-repair conversion, a NICET-qualified
  employee who will stay, a renewal calendar (even on paper), multi-year customer tenure.
- **#1 Stage-2 diligence priority:** the **life-safety liability/claims tail** — pull loss
  runs and confirm affordable tail E&O coverage — paired with verifying local
  certified-inspector labor depth.

### 2. Water Treatment / Cooling-Tower Chemical Service (mean 7.24)
- **Why:** **very sticky** multi-year recurring service-plus-chemical contracts (switching
  risks equipment damage and compliance lapses); essential; technical moat held by
  trained/licensed employees; national majors (Veolia/Nalco, ChemTreat) ignore mid-market.
- **Target sub-segment / size band:** **commercial closed-loop / cooling-tower (HVAC)**
  treatment, **~$1.5M–$3.0M revenue / ~$400K–$700K SDE**, contract-heavy, dense metro
  corridors — **avoid heavy-industrial boiler/process** work (more environmental load).
- **Green flags:** contracted (not at-will) accounts, recurring chemical resale, a trained
  service team, clean chemical-handling practices.
- **#1 Stage-2 diligence priority:** the **environmental/liability tail** — enforcement
  history, Phase I/II, and whether pollution + tail coverage and an environmental escrow can
  be priced sanely; if not, pass on that specific deal.

### 3. Commercial Refrigeration Service (mean 7.20)
- **Why:** **essential cold assets** customers can't let stay down → urgent, high-margin
  emergency work anchored by recurring PM contracts; **residential-HVAC PE skips commercial
  refrigeration**, leaving the sub-$5M tier fragmented and sleepy.
- **Target sub-segment / size band:** **sub-$5M local independents, ~$400K–$900K SDE,
  PM-contract-heavy with an apprentice pipeline**, in metros where CoolSys (the one active
  commercial-refrigeration roll-up) isn't buying.
- **Green flags:** high share of recurring PM-contract revenue (vs pure break-fix), tenured
  EPA-608 techs, grocery/cold-storage anchor accounts with diversified tail.
- **#1 Stage-2 diligence priority:** verify the **recurring-PM-vs-break-fix mix and renewal
  rate** alongside the **local EPA-608 technician supply/retention** (the binding industry
  constraint).

## Close 4th and the MAYBE
- **Calibration & Test-Equipment Lab (ISO 17025) — mean 7.2, PURSUE on judgment.** Mandated
  recall-cycle revenue + accreditation moat; held back by the thin accredited-metrology-tech
  labor pool. **#1 Stage-2 priority: accreditation continuity through the sale** (pre-clear
  the ownership-change transfer with A2LA/ANAB; lock the quality manager + signatory
  metrologists; condition close on accreditation survival). A strong alternative if any of
  the top 3 doesn't yield deal flow.
- **Niche Industrial Distribution / VMI — mean 7.2, MAYBE.** Stickiest customers and the
  purest software-modernization gap in the set, BUT a **structural working-capital drag**
  (inventory + AR) pulls the typical-deal DSCR to ~1.21x — below the 1.25x floor — and owner
  income below the ladder for the commodity midpoint. Becomes a PURSUE only in a narrower
  sub-segment: **specialty / spec'd-SKU distribution** (aerospace/medical-grade fasteners,
  pricing-power parts), higher-turn, **~$800K–$900K SDE**, genuinely VMI-embedded, structured
  with a substantial **standby seller note**. **#1 Stage-2 priority: true working-capital gap
  and inventory health** (turns by SKU, dead stock, cash-conversion cycle, AR aging).

## How the 3 PURSUE industries differ as TYPES
- **Fire & Life-Safety** — *mandated inspection routes*; revenue forced by code; hyper-local
  licensing moat; asset-light. Risk = liability tail.
- **Water Treatment** — *recurring chemical-service contracts*; revenue from switching-cost
  stickiness + consumable resale; technical moat. Risk = environmental tail.
- **Commercial Refrigeration** — *emergency + PM dispatch service*; revenue from essential
  uptime; consolidator-skipped niche. Risk = skilled-tech scarcity.
