# Industry One-Pager (Stage-1 Industry Screen)

## The industry / business type
**Commercial fire & life-safety inspection / testing** — independent companies that perform
the **code-mandated periodic inspection, testing, and maintenance (ITM)** of fire-protection
systems in commercial buildings: fire extinguishers, fire alarms, sprinkler systems,
backflow/standpipes, emergency/exit lighting, kitchen-hood suppression. NFPA codes and local
fire marshals **legally require these inspections on fixed schedules** (annual, quarterly,
monthly), with documentation filed. Revenue is recurring per-inspection fees across a book of
buildings, plus the repair/replacement work that inspections generate ("inspect → find
deficiency → fix").

## Why this is a buy-and-modernize target type
A large, fragmented population of these firms are owned by retiring operators with a recurring
inspection book and no modern systems or marketing. Demand is **forced by law and re-occurs on
a deadline** — about as durable as recurring revenue gets — and **hyper-local licensing/
certification (state fire licenses, NICET)** keeps national consolidators out of the small/
mid tier. (NOTE: the LARGE end of fire-safety IS consolidating via national platforms — the
SBA-tier independent below their buy-box is the target.)

## Revenue mechanism (why it's durable)
Legally-mandated recurring inspection/testing contracts (a renewal book that re-occurs by
code), plus non-discretionary deficiency repairs the inspections surface.

## Operating model (typical)
Licensed/certified inspectors (NICET; state fire license held by the company/qualifying
employee), schedulers/dispatch, and an owner running sales and the inspection calendar. Field
work by the crew; owner-operator manages. Licenses are bridgeable via a qualifying employee.

## The "sleepy" picture (modernization upside across the industry)
Typical operators run **paper inspection tickets, no inspection/asset software, no automated
renewal pipeline, no SEO/online lead capture, no CRM**. Inspection-reporting software (e.g.,
BuildOps/Inspect Point/ServiceTrade class), automated renewal scheduling, deficiency-to-repair
conversion workflows, and digital marketing are broadly open. The founders' compliance edge
fits the documentation/filing side directly.

## Competitive structure
Highly fragmented local operators behind a licensing moat; national fire platforms consolidate
the large tier, leaving the SBA tier sleepy and not bid up.
