**Funnel:** 60 target profiles generated → coarse triage to 12 → fine triage (distinctness-
enforced) to 5 → full five-expert acquisition council + mandatory red-team on each finalist.

**Bar (SHORTLIST):** mean of five expert averages ≥ 8.0 AND no sub-score < 7 AND objectives ≥ 80.

## Result: 0 of 5 finalists clear the bar — an HONEST run
The field clustered at a post-red-team mean of **6.0–6.6**, with no packet manufactured to
8.1. Per CLAUDE.md, a rigorous run that finds zero qualifiers is a SUCCESS, not a failure.
All five are legitimate **RE-TRADE** candidates — good businesses that pencil at a lower,
QoE-verified price with transition contracted in — but none is a "buy at asking" shortlist
qualifier on honest, blind, red-teamed scores.

## The pattern across all five (why the bar wasn't cleared)
The same three dimensions capped every finalist — and they are STRUCTURAL to buying a
sleepy boomer business, not flaws in these particular targets:
1. **Transferability / owner-dependency (COO).** Every seller IS substantially the
   business — top salesperson, scheduler, key relationships, undocumented process. Owner-
   independence and process-maturity scored 2–5 across the board. This is the single
   biggest buy-side risk and it is universal in this seller cohort.
2. **Earnings quality not yet verified (CFO).** Every SDE is seller-asserted with
   add-backs; earnings-quality capped at 6 pending a quality-of-earnings review. Honest
   scoring cannot grant 8 on unverified numbers — which is correct.
3. **Inherited systems / data debt & cybersecurity (CTO + COO).** Paper-and-tribal-
   knowledge operations score 3–5 on inherited-systems condition and 4 on cybersecurity —
   the SAME gap that creates the huge modernization UPSIDE also IS real inherited debt.

The flip side: **modernization upside scored 8–9 on every finalist** (the founders' edge is
real and consistent), and **SBA eligibility scored 9** everywhere. The thesis is sound; the
bar is just (correctly) strict about unverified earnings and owner-dependency at the
profile stage.

## Ranked near-misses + the single cheapest experiment to move each toward GO
1. **F1 Document Shredding (6.56, obj 69)** — best overall: highest mean + objectives, and
   the founders' SOC2/HIPAA compliance background is a *direct* operating moat, not generic
   marketing. *Cheapest experiment:* a quality-of-earnings tie-out separating recurring
   service revenue from commodity recycled-paper resale, + confirm what % of revenue is
   under auto-renewing contract vs the owner's personal relationships. If recurring/
   contracted SDE verifies at ~3.0x, this re-trades to a GO.
2. **F4 Commercial Refrigeration (6.24, obj 65)** — strongest demand + competitive position
   (essential cold assets, PE skips commercial). *Cheapest experiment:* pull the top-10
   customer concentration and the EPA-608 tech roster + tenure; if no customer >15% and the
   lead techs sign stay agreements, transferability and DSCR confidence rise materially.
3. **F5 Portable Sanitation (6.28, obj 61)** — best hard-asset collateral + raw SDE.
   *Cheapest experiment:* verify SDE survives a true fleet-replacement CapEx reserve
   (~$100K/yr) and confirm rolling stock isn't separately priced above the asking number;
   if distributable cash clears debt service at ≥1.4x after the reserve, re-trade to GO.
4. **F3 Backflow Testing (6.44, obj 54)** — purest mandated-recurring demand, but smallest
   (income ceiling) and most owner-dependent. *Cheapest experiment:* confirm the renewal
   database is exportable as clean data AND a certified tester (not the owner) can hold the
   cert; best pursued as a **bolt-on roll-up platform**, not a standalone.
5. **F2 Uniform & Linen (6.00, obj 60)** — durable contracted book, but capital-intensive
   (laundry plant, textile working capital, CapEx). *Cheapest experiment:* decide
   plant-vs-outsourced-wash and quantify the textile working-capital cycle; only attractive
   at ~3.0x of QoE-verified SDE with the plant CERCLA tail diligenced.

## How the five finalists differ (customer + revenue mechanism + deal structure)
- **F1 Shredding** — compliance-driven *destruction route* (HIPAA), commodity-resale layer; light-asset goodwill/route deal; founders' compliance edge.
- **F2 Linen** — *owned-inventory rental route* (textiles), switching-cost stickiness; asset + working-capital-heavy deal.
- **F3 Backflow** — *mandated annual compliance inspection* renewal book; light-asset goodwill deal; smallest, roll-up platform.
- **F4 Refrigeration** — *equipment PM + urgent emergency service* on essential cold assets for grocery/cold-storage; skilled-tech service deal.
- **F5 Portable Sanitation** — *owned-fleet rental + service* into construction/events; capital-intensive hard-asset deal.
