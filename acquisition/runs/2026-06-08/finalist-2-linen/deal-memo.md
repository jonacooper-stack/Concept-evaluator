ONE-PARAGRAPH DEAL READ
This is a genuinely durable, essential, low-concentration recurring book — ~90%+ weekly
contracted route revenue across hundreds of small accounts — that fits the mandate's
"boring, sleepy, modernizable" archetype almost perfectly on the customer-and-demand
axis. But it is NOT a clean GO at the ask. Three of the five experts (CFO, COO, Legal)
land on RE-TRADE, and the two GOs (CMO, CTO) are explicitly conditional. The cash flow is
seller-asserted at a rich ~23% SDE margin with three concurrent capital tails (textiles,
plant, fleet) living below the SDE line; the price (3.5× asserted / ~4.0× normalized)
carries no margin of safety; the entire revenue base rides on retaining route sales reps
(RSRs) who personally hold the customer relationships; and the in-house laundry plant (if
present) drags an environmental/CERCLA tail. This is a real business and a real thesis —
but it is a RE-TRADE on price and structure, not a shortlist qualifier on today's numbers.

most aggressively on earnings quality, DSCR, transferability, and retention.

   independent contract forms are UNREAD — auto-renew enforceability and assignability are
   assumed, not verified, and CMO flags that an unknown share may be at-will/handshake. A
   durability score of 8 requires the contracts to actually be enforceable and assignable;
   on unread paper that is a 7, not an 8. LANDS. Lower to 7. CFO avg: 6.0 → 5.9.

   6.7 → 6.6.

WHERE THE EXPERTS AGREE (high signal)
- The recurring, contracted, low-concentration book is genuinely durable — every expert
  credits the ~90% weekly route revenue across hundreds of accounts with no customer >~10%.
- The pricing lever (years of un-raised prices vs. industry 3–6% escalators) is the single
  highest-ROI move — CFO, CMO, COO, and CTO all independently size it near $110K–$225K.
- Route-rep (RSR) retention is THE load-bearing transition risk — CFO, CMO, and COO all
  name RSR flight as the single point of revenue failure; the relationships are in the
  drivers' heads, not the company's.
- The price carries no margin of safety at 3.5× asserted / ~4.0× normalized (CFO explicit;
  Legal and COO both condition GO on price/holdback adjustment).

WHERE THE EXPERTS DISAGREE
- GO vs RE-TRADE split (CMO/CTO GO; CFO/COO/Legal RE-TRADE). The underlying assumption:
  the two GOs scope their verdict to their own lane (customer-base durability; tech
  modernization path) and explicitly hand the price/earnings/transition risk to others —
  CMO says "underwrite to pricing + retention + ops," CTO says "downgrade to RE-TRADE if
  data won't reconcile." The three RE-TRADEs own the cross-cutting deal risks (unverified
  SDE, DSCR fragility, environmental tail, RSR retention). MY CALL: the RE-TRADE frame is
  load-bearing. A route-rental deal lives or dies on earnings quality + RSR retention +
  price, exactly the CFO/COO axis — and the GOs are conditional GOs that collapse into
  RE-TRADE the moment their own conditions (assignable contracts; reconcilable data) fail
  in diligence. The honest synthesis is RE-TRADE.
- Magnitude of modernization upside (COO/CTO 9 vs CMO 6). Underlying assumption: whether
  the founders' edge is digital marketing (CMO: narrow here) or ops/pricing/systems
  (COO/CTO: large). LOAD-BEARING CALL: CMO is right that this is NOT a paid-acquisition
  turnaround — the upside is pricing discipline + routing + portal + billing-leakage
  recovery, which is ops/tech-led. The founders' marketing edge is the cherry, not the
  cake. This matters because it means the value case rests on operational execution against
  un-reconstructed data, which is riskier than a clean marketing flywheel.

TOP 5 RISKS (RANKED)
1. Unverified earnings / capital tail below the SDE line (CFO) — the ~23% margin is rich;
   under-reserved textile replacement + deferred plant/boiler/water-treatment + aging
   fleet can mean true SDE is $550K–$575K, pushing the multiple to ~4.0× and conservative
   DSCR to 1.42x. Reduces with a full QoE tying SDE to 3 yrs of returns + a textile/equipment
   inspection + price re-trade to ~3.0× of verified SDE.
2. RSR retention through transition (COO, CMO, CFO) — a 15% stop defection (~$420K) vaporizes
   the DSCR cushion AND the modernization thesis simultaneously; no stay agreements exist.
   Reduces with named, dollar-quantified RSR stay bonuses through the first renewal cycle
   and a 6–12 month seller transition converting big accounts to company relationships.
3. Environmental / CERCLA tail of an in-house laundry plant (Legal) — wastewater discharge
   + solvent/oil/metals residue from mechanic uniforms and shop towels; successor liability
   follows the operator regardless of asset structure; remediation can run six-to-seven
   figures. Reduces with a mandatory Phase I (Phase II if flagged), environmental rep/indemnity
   backed by escrow or environmental insurance, and confirming in-house vs. outsourced wash.
4. DSCR fragility under combined rate + transition-churn shock (CFO) — drops to ~1.02x under
   +200bps and −15% revenue, below the 1.25x floor; the cushion only exists if both the
   earnings hold and the reps stay. Reduces with a price re-trade (lower debt), a standby
   seller note, and verified earnings.
5. Un-reconstructable operational data / contract assignability (CTO, Legal) — items-in-service,
   per-account pricing, and contract renewal dates live on paper and in reps'/owner's heads;
   if they can't reconcile to billing, the price-increase and billing-leakage thesis becomes
   guesswork and the modernization timeline stretches from months to a year-plus. Reduces by
   sampling 10 accounts in diligence (counts vs. last 3 invoices) and having counsel read the
   actual contract forms for assignability/auto-renew before close.

TOP 3 STRENGTHS
- Genuinely recurring, contracted, low-concentration revenue (~90%+ weekly, hundreds of
  accounts, none >~10%) with real textile-ownership + wash-cycle switching costs — the
  durable cash-flow shape the mandate targets.
- A concrete, near-pure-margin pricing lever: years of un-raised prices vs. an industry
  norm of 3–6% escalators, independently sized near $110K–$225K by four of five experts.
- Favorable competitive structure and clean SBA profile: the big three ignore small accounts
  and independents don't bid each other up (no PE roll-up war), with hard pledgeable
  collateral (plant equipment, fleet, textile inventory) and no owner-credential gate.

THE BIGGEST OPEN QUESTION
Is the asserted $650K SDE real after an honest textile-replacement reserve and plant/fleet
CapEx — and is laundering in-house or outsourced? These two facts decide the deal. If a QoE
confirms reserve-adjusted SDE near $650K AND the wash is outsourced (collapsing the
environmental tail), the price re-trade to ~3.0× plus RSR stay agreements likely converts
this to a GO with comfortable DSCR. If the textile reserve was starved (true SDE sub-$550K)
OR a Phase I/II on an owned in-house plant surfaces contamination, the multiple balloons,
DSCR breaks under stress, and the environmental liability is uncontainable — turning a
RE-TRADE into a NO-GO. Everything else (RSR retention, data reconstruction) is structurable;
these two are existential.

RECOMMENDATION: RE-TRADE

IF RE-TRADE — what would make this a GO:
- Price to ~$1.95M–$2.0M (≈3.0× a QoE-verified, reserve-adjusted SDE), not $2.3M (3.5×
  asserted). Addresses CFO's no-margin-of-safety call; verified by a quality-of-earnings
  tie-out to 3 years of tax returns and the year-by-year textile-replacement spend.
- A ~$115K standby seller note inside the 10% injection (SBA full-standby), preserving
  founder reserves and doubling as an indemnity clawback. Addresses CFO + Legal.
- Named, dollar-quantified RSR stay bonuses for every route rep through at least the first
  renewal cycle, plus a 6–12 month paid seller transition with warm introductions to the
  top ~20 accounts and full handover of the pricing book and contract calendar. Addresses
  COO + CMO's load-bearing retention risk; verified via rep tenure/comp review and signed
  agreements as conditions of close.
- Mandatory Phase I ESA (Phase II if flagged) with an environmental rep/indemnity backed by
  escrow or environmental insurance, plus confirmation of in-house vs. outsourced wash.
  Addresses Legal's biggest single risk.
- 10–15% escrow/holdback for 12–18 months with environmental, tax (sales/use on rental
  revenue), employment (FLSA route-rep classification), and contract-assignability reps.
  Addresses Legal's successor-liability tail.
- Diligence confirmation that items-in-service/pricing/contract data is exportable and
  reconcilable to billing within an acceptable error rate (sample 10 accounts). Addresses
  CTO; if it fails, the modernization scope and price must be re-cut further.

At ~3.0× of verified SDE with the standby note, RSR retention locked, a clean (or outsourced)
wash, and the escrow package, base DSCR sits comfortably ~1.7x+, the deal clears the income
ladder for the founders with modernization upside on top, and three of five RE-TRADEs
convert to GO. It becomes a NO-GO only if QoE shows true SDE sub-$550K or a Phase II surfaces
material contamination on an owned plant.
