DEAL MEMO — Commercial Refrigeration Service Company (~$3.2M rev / ~$720K SDE)
Date: 2026-06-08
Council reviewed: CFO, CMO, COO, Legal/Regulatory, CTO

ONE-PARAGRAPH DEAL READ
This is a genuinely attractive niche on the page — non-discretionary, spoilage-driven demand; recurring PM contracts; a fragmented field that residential-HVAC PE roll-ups (Wrench Group, Apex, Leap) skip; and an unusually clean, founder-executable modernization wedge (FSM software, IoT remote-monitoring service plans, B2B digital). But it does NOT clear the shortlist bar, and the reason is structural, not cosmetic: the deal's entire cash flow rests on two things the council cannot yet verify and two things it can already see are weak. The owner personally carries sales, anchor-account relationships, AND dispatch — three roles that vanish on close — while the customer/equipment book lives in his head with no system of record; and the deliverable capacity sits in a scarce, aging crew of ~8 EPA-608 techs at ~78% utilization. The SDE almost certainly overstates transferable cash flow (subtract a ~$135K market owner salary and base DSCR drops from headline comfort to 1.61x, and to 1.21x under a combined rate-and-churn shock). Honest verdict: a real RE-TRADE candidate — a fine deal at a lower price with the transition contracted in — but a NEAR-MISS against an 8.0 / no-sub-score-under-7 shortlist standard.

Underlying assumption: COO frames the triple owner-role replacement + scarce-crew retention as a day-one cliff that must be contracted into the deal or the cash flow doesn't show up; the GO camp assumes those mitigations (seller transition, stay agreements, contract consents) WILL be structured in. **My call: COO's frame is load-bearing.** On an owner-relationship-and-crew-dependent service book, the deal lives or dies on transition execution, not on the (attractive) modernization thesis. TOP 3 STRENGTHS

- A rare, concrete, founder-executable modernization wedge: FSM software recaptures ~10-20% billable hours on the existing scarce crew (≈1-1.5 techs without hiring) and IoT remote-monitoring opens a genuinely NEW high-margin recurring line.
- Fragmented niche that residential-HVAC PE consolidators (Wrench Group, Apex, Leap) skip and national OEMs (Source/EMCOR, Hussmann) ignore at the mid-market — no bidding war, multiple-arbitrage room intact; and the EPA-608 credential is employee-held and bridgeable.

THE BIGGEST OPEN QUESTION
What share of the $3.2M is contracted PM revenue on written, assignable agreements versus owner-relationship handshake renewals and at-will T&M — and how concentrated is it in the top accounts? This single answer decides everything downstream: if contracted share is high (~50%+), the anchors are facility-level contracts with assignment language, and the top customer is under ~25%, then transition risk collapses to a structuring exercise and this is a clean GO at or near $2.5M. If contracted share is low and the anchors are the owner's personal handshakes with one customer >30%, the recurring-revenue thesis is illusory, base DSCR is one churn event away from breaching the floor that backs the founders' full personal guarantee, and price must come down materially. Everything else (modernization, financeability, eligibility) is favorable; this is the variable the verdict turns on.

RECOMMENDATION: RE-TRADE

IF RE-TRADE (the path to GO):
- **Price.** If QoE haircuts SDE below ~$580K OR top-customer concentration is >30% on owner-held relationships, re-trade from $2.5M / 3.5x toward **~$2.1M-$2.25M (≈3.0x)** to restore DSCR cushion and de-risk the PG. Addresses CFO earnings-quality and DSCR-fragility concerns; verified by QoE + customer-revenue schedule.
- **Structure.** Push a larger slice of price into a **standby seller note and/or a revenue-retention earnout tied to anchor-account retention through month 18** — lowers debt service AND aligns the seller to the handoff. Addresses CFO + CMO + Legal transfer risk; verified by the retention metric itself.
- **Transition as closing conditions, not assumptions.** (1) 6-month paid seller transition + tapering on-call tail to month 12 with written handover of accounts/dispatch/vendors; (2) signed stay agreements with lead + senior EPA-608 techs (~$10-20K each); (3) written assignment/consent on material PM and anchor accounts; (4) a closing condition that verified EPA-608 headcount, top-customer concentration, and PM/T&M split match representations. Addresses COO (load-bearing) + Legal + CTO; verified at close.
- **Compliance gates.** Phase I ESA, CAA §608 refrigerant-compliance audit, contractor qualifying-party substitution, R&W + 10-15% escrow (12-18 mo), state tax clearances before close. Addresses Legal tail.
- If those land, it converts to a GO; the most important first-90-day move is the knowledge-extraction + crew-and-anchor stabilization, and the month-6 proof metric is **contracted-PM revenue retained vs. at-close baseline (target ≥95%)**.
