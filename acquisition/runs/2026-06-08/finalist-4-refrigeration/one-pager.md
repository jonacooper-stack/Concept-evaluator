# Acquisition Target One-Pager

## The business
A **commercial refrigeration service company** serving grocery stores, convenience stores,
restaurants, food processors, cold-storage warehouses, and similar food-related customers.
It provides **recurring preventive-maintenance (PM) contracts** on refrigeration and
cold-storage systems (walk-in coolers/freezers, display cases, compressor racks,
ice machines) plus **emergency break-fix service** when essential cold assets fail —
where downtime means spoiled inventory, so response is urgent and non-discretionary.
Revenue is scheduled PM contract revenue plus time-and-materials emergency/repair calls.

## Why it's available
The owner is **retiring** and has a crew of EPA-608-certified refrigeration technicians in
place, but never built management systems or marketing.

## Representative financials [ASSUMED — verify with QoE / tax returns / 3–5 comps]
- Revenue: **~$3.2M/yr**, a mix of recurring PM contracts and emergency/repair work.
- SDE: **~$720K/yr**, stable to growing with food-retail demand.
- Asking price: **~$2.5M (~3.5× SDE)** [ASSUMED].
- Indicative SBA 7(a) structure: ~10% equity injection, 10-yr term, ~10.5% rate, personal
  guarantee; service trucks, tools, and some parts inventory are collateral.
- Customer concentration: moderate — a few anchor grocery/cold-storage accounts may be
  meaningful; the rest is diversified. [VERIFY top-customer %.]

## Revenue mechanism
Recurring PM/service-agreement contracts (scheduled, contracted) anchoring a larger base of
**urgent, high-margin emergency repair** revenue on essential cold assets customers cannot
let stay down.

## Operating shape
A team of **EPA-608-certified refrigeration technicians** (the certification is held by
employees, transferable/hireable), dispatch/office staff, and the owner, who currently runs
sales, key accounts, and scheduling. The work is field-based but **managed** — the buyer
runs the company and the techs do the trade. Skilled-tech recruiting/retention is the
central operating constraint.

## Current go-to-market & systems (the "sleepy" picture)
Customers come from **long-standing relationships and referrals**; **no dispatch/field-
service software**, no remote monitoring upsell, no CRM, no digital marketing. Scheduling
and invoicing are manual. Residential-HVAC-focused PE consolidators largely **skip
commercial refrigeration**, leaving the niche fragmented and un-modernized. Modern field-
service management, IoT remote-monitoring service plans, and B2B marketing are open upside.

## What a buyer would be taking on
Retaining the certified technicians and the anchor accounts through transition; replacing
the owner's sales/dispatch role; verifying customer concentration; deploying field-service
software, remote-monitoring service plans, and marketing; and managing skilled-labor
scarcity and after-hours emergency-service operations.
