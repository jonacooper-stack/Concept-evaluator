ONE-PARAGRAPH DEAL READ
This is a legally-mandated, recession-proof recurring-revenue book — about as durable a
demand profile as Main-Street gets — wrapped around an operation that runs entirely out
of one retiring man's head and a filing cabinet, where that same man is the lead
certified tester actually performing the billable work. The certified-tester credential
is legally bridgeable (an employee can hold it, which doc-01 #5 permits), so this is not
a hard-constraint kill; but the credential AND the route knowledge AND the
water-authority filing relationships all walk out the door bundled in one person, and the
seller's $240K SDE almost certainly double-counts a tester salary the buyer must now pay.
Strip that out and DSCR drops from a comfortable 1.52x to 0.76–1.14x — below the 1.25x
lender floor — and a single founder nets ~$135K, far short of the $300K ladder, with no
capacity to carry two founders. The honest verdict is that this is a RE-TRADE platform
candidate at a lower price, not a clean GO at $720K, and it does not clear the shortlist
bar on absolute merit.

WHERE THE EXPERTS DISAGREE (and my call on whose frame is load-bearing)
- Recommendation split: CMO/Legal/CTO say GO (conditioned); CFO/COO say RE-TRADE. The
  GO camp is scoring the QUALITY OF THE ASSET (durable demand, clean eligibility, clean
  tech fit); the RE-TRADE camp is scoring the PRICE AND TRANSFER (SDE inflated by tester
  labor, owner-dependency 2). For THIS target the load-bearing frame is CFO+COO: a
  goodwill-only book "run out of his head" lives or dies on whether the cash flow
  transfers at the assumed multiple, not on how good the asset would be if it did. The
  GO recommendations are themselves conditioned on the exact diligence that the RE-TRADE
  camp says must reprice the deal — so they are closer than they look. RE-TRADE wins.
- Normalized SDE: CFO models a $160–240K band; CMO/Legal accept the ~$240K headline. The
  underlying assumption is how much of the owner's time was BILLABLE testing vs.
  management. CFO's frame is load-bearing: SDE adds back ONE owner's comp, and this owner
  is GM + lead tester, so part of the add-back is an operating cost in disguise. Until
  QoE resolves the hour split, the conservative band governs.
- Owner-independence severity: COO scores 2; CMO scores brand-vs-owner 4. COO is more
  load-bearing — it correctly counts the credential, the route knowledge, the filing
  calendar, AND the authority relationships as four single points of failure in one
  departing human, where CMO partly assumes the book conveys.

THE BIGGEST OPEN QUESTION
What fraction of the owner's $240K SDE is actually transferable once a certified replacement
tester is paid, and does the renewal book exist as clean, exportable, assembly-level data
that conveys at close? These two unknowns are the same question wearing two hats — both ask
whether you are buying a transferable recurring engine or one retiring man's memory and
labor. If QoE shows the owner was mostly managing with an employed tester who stays AND the
renewal list exports cleanly, normalized SDE holds near $220–240K, DSCR clears 1.4x+, and
this becomes a defensible RE-TRADE-to-GO at a fair price. If the owner personally tested a
large share of the route and the book lives in his head, normalized SDE falls toward $160K,
DSCR sinks below the floor, and the deal is dead at $720K and marginal even repriced.

RECOMMENDATION: RE-TRADE

IF RE-TRADE — the specific changes that would move it toward GO:
- PRICE: re-trade from ~$720K (3.0x stated SDE) to ~2.5x on a QoE-NORMALIZED SDE. If QoE
  confirms ~$200K normalized, that is ~$500K, not $720K — addresses CFO's tester-labor and
  DSCR concerns (restores DSCR to ~1.4x+).
- STRUCTURE: a meaningful seller note on full SBA standby (toward the 10% injection) plus a
  10–15% escrow/holdback (12–18 mo) and an earnout tied to renewal-book retention through
  the first full annual test cycle — addresses CMO/COO retention risk; the seller is paid
  as the book proves it transfers.
- CLOSING CONDITIONS (not post-close hopes): (a) a confirmed retained second certified
  tester on a stay agreement OR an identified credentialed hire in place; (b) a complete,
  audited, exportable renewal database delivered at close; (c) a 6–12 month seller
  transition-consulting agreement with ride-alongs and warm authority/PM introductions;
  (d) water-authority re-registration of the new entity pre-staged. Addresses COO/Legal/CTO.
- DILIGENCE THAT VERIFIES EACH: QoE on billable-owner-hours; a renewal-list audit (count
  assemblies with complete records; reconcile tested-vs-due for lapse rate); a CPA
  worker-classification audit; per-authority registration-transfer confirmation.
- POSTURE: buy ONLY as an explicit modernization-and-bolt-on PLATFORM. As a standalone
  income vehicle it fails the founder ladder for even one founder and cannot carry two.
