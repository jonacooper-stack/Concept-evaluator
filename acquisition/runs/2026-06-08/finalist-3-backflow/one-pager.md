# Acquisition Target One-Pager

## The business
A specialized **backflow-prevention testing and cross-connection-control compliance
company**. Most US water purveyors legally require that backflow-prevention assemblies on
commercial, industrial, irrigation, and multi-family properties be **tested and certified
annually** (and repaired/rebuilt when they fail) by a certified tester, with results filed
to the local water authority. The company maintains a **book of properties** whose
assemblies it tests every year, generates the certifications, files them with the
authorities, and performs repairs/replacements when devices fail. Revenue is the per-test
fee across a large recurring renewal base, plus repair/rebuild/replacement work.

## Why it's available
The owner — the lead certified tester who built the route — is **nearing retirement**. The
business runs largely out of his head and a filing cabinet.

## Revenue mechanism
Legally-mandated **annual recurring testing/certification** (a renewal book — every device
must be re-tested each year), plus non-discretionary repair/replacement when a device
fails its test.

## Operating shape
One or two certified backflow testers (state/water-authority certification), a scheduler,
and the owner. The work is field-based (a tester drives a route of properties) but
**managed** — the buyer would employ certified testers, not necessarily be one. The core
asset is the **annual renewal list** and the relationships with property managers and
water authorities.

## Current go-to-market & systems (the "sleepy" picture)
Entirely **paper/spreadsheet-driven**: the renewal list, scheduling, certificates, and
authority filings are manual; **no automated renewal-reminder pipeline**, no online
booking, no CRM, no review presence or SEO. Customers are won by being the incumbent
tester and by water-authority referral. Competitors are **solo certified testers** with no
marketing. The certification barrier keeps national consolidators out of the niche.

## What a buyer would be taking on
Ensuring certified-tester capacity transfers (employing/retaining a qualified tester so the
owner's personal certification isn't the single point of failure); converting the manual
renewal list into an automated recurring-renewal engine; and growing the book via the
modernization the incumbent never did. Small absolute size means the income ceiling depends
on growth and/or bolt-ons.
