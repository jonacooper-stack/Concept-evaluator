## Key findings
- **Top of field = mandated-compliance recurring-inspection businesses** (backflow,
  fire/life-safety, crane, calibration, elevator, water treatment): recurring revenue is
  legally *required*; niche certification barrier keeps national PE out; transfers on a
  licensed/certified *employee* (bridgeable); universally paper/no-CRM = clean
  high-ROI modernization wedge. Dead-center of the founders' thesis. NOTE: these are
  near-duplicates in SHAPE — the fine cut must enforce distinctness.
- **Route-density service** (linen #3, shredding #6, porta-john #9, refrigeration #24)
  rounds out the 12: sticky contracted/route books, fragmented sleepy fields.

  revenue, prices of $6.5–9M exceed the $5M SBA 7(a) loan cap; flagged for restructuring.
