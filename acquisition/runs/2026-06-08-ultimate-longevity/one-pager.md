# Concept Evaluation One-Pager — Ultimate Longevity Center (franchise)

> NOTE ON FRAMING: This is NOT a sleepy-business acquisition (our usual thesis). It is a
> **brand-new franchise startup** — buying the right to BUILD a new unit. There is no existing
> cash flow to buy, no operating history, and (being this new) no FDD Item 19 earnings track
> record. Evaluate it as a **franchise-startup bet on the longevity macro trend**, with the
> economics judged against the closest public comparable. The council should weigh ECONOMIC
> VIABILITY (CFO) and DEMAND SIGNALS vs. the demographic/macro backdrop (CMO) most heavily.

## What it is
**Ultimate Longevity Center** — a new brick-and-mortar "longevity / preventative wellness"
franchise. Each location is a physical destination built around three integrated zones
(per the brand's materials):
- **The Playground** — experiential recovery modalities: red-light therapy, hyperbaric oxygen,
  cold plunge, hydrogen bath, etc.
- **The Lab** — biomarker/blood testing and "personalized longevity protocols."
- **The Apothecary** — clinically-informed supplements and therapies (retail).
Revenue model (typical of the category): recurring **memberships** + à-la-carte services +
**diagnostics** + **supplement/retail**, plus an "affiliate ecosystem." Full-time
owner-operator required.

## Who is behind it
- **Sequel Brands / Anthony Geisler** — the franchisor operator. Geisler built and scaled Club
  Pilates, CycleBar, Row House (the Xponential Fitness boutique-fitness roll-up). Serious
  franchise-scaling pedigree. [FLAG: Xponential also drew franchisee-profitability complaints,
  litigation, and a short-seller report — a relevant track-record caveat, not a verdict.]
- **Gary Brecka** — "human biologist"/biohacker who developed the longevity protocols. A
  high-reach but **scientifically contested** celebrity figure (his claims have drawn
  criticism) → brand and regulatory/reputational exposure.
- **Tony Robbins / Lifeforce** — brand and affiliate backing.
- Status: **very new** — public materials say it has "reached its current goals… more
  opportunities coming soon," i.e., early-stage rollout with few/no seasoned units.

## The numbers from the listing
- Franchise fee: **$65K**. Total investment: **$508,778 – $1,182,187**. Liquid capital:
  **$150K min**. Lifestyle: **full-time** owner-operator.
- Royalty / marketing fees: **not stated in the listing** [ASSUMED ~6–8% royalty + ~2%
  marketing, by category norm — verify in the FDD].
- SBA note: SBA *can* finance franchise startups if the brand is SBA-eligible and the projected
  cash flow supports it — but there is **no proven cash flow**, so any DSCR is a *projection*,
  not a fact.

## Demand / macro backdrop (sourced — the tailwind is real)
- **Global wellness economy: ~$6.8T in 2024 → ~$9.8T by 2029** (~7.6%/yr) — Global Wellness
  Institute.
- **"Longevity economy" framed at ~$8T** globally in the brand's own marketing; the narrower
  **longevity market ~$31.6B in 2026 → ~$46.9B by 2031 (~8.2%/yr)** — Mordor Intelligence.
- Preventive-healthcare and "healthspan" spending growing; demographic tailwind from aging
  Boomers PLUS affluent younger "biohacker" demand and the GLP-1/wellness cultural moment.
- These are **discretionary, premium** dollars — real and growing, but cyclical and
  recession-sensitive.

## The closest public comparable (the reality check) — Restore Hyper Wellness
A directly analogous cryo/recovery/wellness franchise with a public FDD:
- Average unit volume **~$851K–$962K**; **estimated owner earnings ~$119K–$153K** (~14–16% of
  revenue); investment **~$779K–$1.31M**; **payback ~8.1–10.1 years**.
- **18 unit closures in 2024 (8.6% of ~209 units)**; the franchisor/analysts cite **margin
  compression from over-saturation** in the cryo/wellness-recovery segment.
- Royalty **7–8% + 2% marketing**.
(Source basis for a sanity check on what a mature unit in this category actually earns.)

## The core questions for the council
1. **Economic viability (CFO):** at ~$850K–$1.2M all-in and category-comparable ~$120–150K
   owner earnings, does this service an SBA loan + pay a full-time owner the income-ladder
   target — or is the payback 8–10 years like the comp? What does DSCR look like on projections?
2. **Demand (CMO):** the macro tailwind is real and large — but is THIS concept (unproven,
   celebrity-driven, premium-discretionary, in an over-saturating recovery-studio segment)
   positioned to capture durable, recurring demand, or is it late-cycle me-too?
3. **Ops (COO):** can two owner-operators run a high-touch, multi-modality, medically-adjacent
   center, staff it (clinical/RN/NP for labs?), and keep utilization high?
4. **Legal (COO/Legal):** medical/diagnostic claims, who orders/interprets labs (MD/telehealth
   oversight), supplement/retail claims, FTC health-claims and state medical-practice exposure,
   plus standard FDD/franchise-agreement terms (royalty, territory, term, transfer).
5. **Tech (CTO):** the "Lab"/protocol/member-app/data stack — is it provided and modern, or a
   franchisee burden; what PHI/data-security obligations come with biomarker testing.
