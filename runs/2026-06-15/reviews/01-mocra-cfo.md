CFO REVIEW — Cosmetics Regulatory Compliance Program (MoCRA)

ONE-PARAGRAPH FINANCIAL READ

The numbers pencil well for a bootstrapped software-leveraged compliance subscription: federally-forced, recurring demand at a $3K–$12K annual price point against a known incumbent alternative (hourly regulatory consultants and law firms at $300–$600/hr), with gross margins that should sit at 70–80% once the platform exists and onboarding/dossier review is the main human cost. The central financial fragility is not the revenue model — it is ARPU-times-customer-count credibility at the income ladder: to put ~$300K/founder ($600K combined income, implying roughly $850K–$1.0M ARR after founder draws and a part-time regulatory advisor) on the table by month 12, the founders need on the order of 130–200 paying brands at a ~$5K–$6K blended ACV inside year one, which is an aggressive but not heroic logo-acquisition pace for a forced, deadline-driven buy. The other watch-item is durability of the forcing function and pricing: MoCRA is genuinely mandatory and recurring, but a chunk of the "submission" work (facility registration, product listing) is a periodic event rather than a continuous burn, so I want to confirm the renewal/retention story rests on the ongoing obligations (adverse-event monitoring, listing currency, GMP rules, retailer gating) and not just the initial filing — otherwise this risks looking like a high-churn "one-and-done filing" product dressed as a subscription.

TOP 3 FINANCIAL STRENGTHS
- Negative working capital + self-funding growth: annual-prepay plus upfront onboarding fee means customers pay before costs land.
- Forced, recurring, switching-costly demand at a defensible price point: federal MoCRA mandate underwrites high gross retention; re-papering a brand's entire FDA posture is a real switching cost; LTV/CAC ~4–9× against $300–$600/hr consultants.
- Thin slice of a large forced-demand market clears the ladder: only ~0.7%–1.1% of a conservative 15K–25K base in year one (~$900K ARR / ~164 brands) reaches ~$300K/founder; low concentration.

TOP 3 FINANCIAL RISKS
- "One-and-done filing" churn risk: if value is perceived as the initial registration/listing event, brands may cancel after year one, collapsing the subscription into a high-churn one-time-fee business.
- ARPU/CAC compression from below: base skews to small indie brands that resist $5K+/yr and DIY the FDA portal; if ACV drifts to $3K–$4K and CAC rises, logo count to hit the ladder jumps to 250–300+ in year one.
- Margin cap and step-cost from the expert layer: safety-dossier review doesn't fully productize; heavier-than-expected onboarding labor pushes margin toward high-60s and forces RA hires.

BIGGEST SINGLE RISK

The single financial issue most likely to kill this is retention/churn after the initial filing — the risk that this is structurally a one-time onboarding fee masquerading as a recurring subscription. MoCRA's most labor-visible obligations (facility registration, initial product/ingredient listing) are front-loaded into the first engagement; renewals are biennial and listing updates intermittent, so a price-sensitive indie brand that has been "set up" may rationally drop the subscription and self-manage the next renewal. If gross retention lands at 55–65% instead of 85–90%, LTV roughly halves, LTV/CAC falls below the comfort line, and the founders are on a logo-replacement treadmill, threatening the month-24 ~$500K/founder rung. The mitigants must be designed in: anchor ongoing value in continuous obligations the brand cannot let lapse — adverse-event intake/15-day reporting and six-year retention, listing currency, forthcoming GMP rules, and especially retailer/marketplace gating that re-checks compliance before stocking — so lapsing has an immediate, visible downside.

QUESTIONS THE FOUNDERS MUST ANSWER BEFORE I'M COMFORTABLE
- What is realistic gross-dollar and logo retention after year one, and which ongoing obligation makes lapsing painful enough to renew? (Interview 15–20 brands + 3–5 retail/marketplace buyers on whether they re-verify MoCRA before stocking.)
- What is true blended ACV once mix is known, and how many will pay $5K+ vs. DIY the free FDA portal? (Run a $2K–$5K paid-search + pricing-page test on "MoCRA compliance.")
- What does onboarding actually cost in expert hours per brand, and does the $1K–$3K fee cover it? (Time-track 3–5 real onboardings.)
- How fast does the "MoCRA" search/awareness demand wave decay, and what is the year-two acquisition channel (contract-manufacturer partnerships, retailer referrals, agency channel)?

The financial structure is genuinely strong for a bootstrapped, capital-light, recurring business: federally-forced demand, negative working capital, ~72–80% gross margin, and a bottoms-up path to ~$300K/founder on ~164 logos (≈1% of a conservative base) defensible with shown math. With that answered affirmatively in a 15–20 customer loop, this clears the ladder with conservative assumptions; score 7.9, GO.
