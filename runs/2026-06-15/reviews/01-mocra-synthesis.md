ONE-PARAGRAPH PM READ
MoCRA compliance-as-a-service is a beautifully built business on a badly contested battlefield. Four of six experts (CFO, COO, CTO, Legal) are right that on their own axes this is excellent: federally-forced recurring demand, negative working capital, an 8–12 week off-the-shelf build, zero field ops, no license required, insurable risk because the founders sit beside the regulated duty. But the two market-facing reviews — especially the dedicated web-researched Competitive Analyst at 4.7 — document that the founders' core thesis (sleepy incumbents, clear air) is factually wrong here: the category is owned above by a PE-backed roll-up (Registrar Corp) that already sells this exact product and owns the software layer, commoditized below by a $4M-funded AI entrant (Certo) and a 10x-cheaper anchor (Cosmetica $199/mo), and floored by a free FDA portal. The score lands honestly at 69/100 (Tier B, bottom of band) because the 18-point competition dimension scores a 4.
