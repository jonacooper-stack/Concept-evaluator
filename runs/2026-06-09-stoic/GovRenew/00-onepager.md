# GovRenew — Clean-Room One-Pager

## What it is
A productized, recurring managed service that keeps a government contractor's
eligibility credentials current and never-lapsed: the annual SAM.gov entity
registration renewal, SBA program recertifications (8(a) annual reviews, HUBZone
recertification, WOSB/EDWOSB and SDVOSB/VOSB recertifications and annual
attestations), small-business size recertifications, state/local vendor
registrations and prequalifications, and the rep-and-cert upkeep that gates
contract awards and payments. Software runs the multi-credential deadline calendar,
the renewal-document assembly, the entity-validation workflow, and the records
archive; a named GovCon compliance specialist reviews eligibility/size questions.

## The customer
Small and mid-size US government contractors — especially the small-disadvantaged,
veteran-owned (SDVOSB/VOSB), women-owned (WOSB/EDWOSB), HUBZone, and 8(a) firms
that depend on set-aside eligibility — typically without a dedicated contracts/
compliance administrator. These firms win and invoice on federal (and state/local)
contracts only while their registrations and certifications are active.

## The pain
Federal contracting eligibility is gated by credentials that expire on hard,
public deadlines: a SAM.gov entity registration must be renewed annually or the
firm cannot receive new awards or, in many cases, payments; SBA set-aside
certifications carry annual attestations and periodic (≈3-year) recertifications,
and a lapse or a botched recertification drops the firm out of the very set-aside
program its pipeline depends on; entity-validation and rep-and-cert updates
routinely trip firms up and freeze their ability to bid. A lapse is directly
revenue-gating — deals stall, payments freeze, set-aside eligibility evaporates —
and the work recurs every year (and on every ownership/size/address change). The
buyer universe and its renewal dates are public on SAM.gov.

## The product / service
1. **Credential calendar & monitoring** — a tracked calendar of SAM renewal, each
   SBA recertification/attestation, and state/local registration dates, with early
   warnings and entity-validation prep.
2. **Renewal & recertification execution** — assemble and submit the SAM renewal,
   SBA recertification packages, state vendor registrations, and rep-and-cert
   updates; manage SBA/entity-validation back-and-forth.
3. **Eligibility review** — a named GovCon specialist reviews size-standard and
   set-aside eligibility questions (ownership, control, size recalculation) before
   anything is attested.
4. **Records archive** — a dated, defensible file of every certification and
   attestation, ready for an SBA program examination or a size protest.

## Revenue model
Recurring annual subscription tiered by the number of certifications/registrations
maintained and entity complexity: [ASSUMED] ~$3,000–$15,000/yr (single-SAM-plus-one-
cert small firm at the low end; multi-cert, multi-state, 8(a)/HUBZone firms at the
high end), with add-on fees for new certification applications and size-protest/
examination support. [ASSUMED] blended ARPU ~$6,000/yr. Recurring because renewals
and attestations are mandatory every year.

## Positioning
"Never lose a contract to a lapsed registration again. We keep your SAM, your
set-aside certifications, and your state registrations current and audit-ready,
every year — so you stay eligible to win and get paid. Fixed annual fee."

## Competitive landscape (stated neutrally)
The space is served by GovCon proposal/capture consultants (who treat
registration/certification as a side task, billed hourly or as part of bid work),
a long tail of small "we'll do your SAM/8(a) application" service shops (mostly
one-time-application-focused, not recurring managed maintenance), and DIY (SAM.gov
registration is free and self-service, though error-prone, and SBA certifications
run through certify.SBA.gov). There is no dominant productized, fixed-fee, recurring
managed service that owns the full multi-credential lifecycle for the small
set-aside contractor.

## Why these founders
- Marketing/positioning to reach a fragmented base of small set-aside contractors
  who fear losing eligibility and dislike the bureaucracy.
- Software to build the multi-credential deadline calendar, renewal-assembly, and
  entity-validation workflow.
- Compliance-operator + regulated-deal instinct to run the program and structure
  the eligibility-review discipline.
- The GovCon-eligibility expertise is a real but bridgeable moat: SAM and SBA rules
  are codified and public, and a named GovCon compliance specialist (hire or
  partner) covers the size/eligibility judgment that carries the real risk.

## Acquisition motion
Buyers and their renewal dates are public on SAM.gov (entity registrations with
expiration dates) and in SBA's DSBS / certification directories; outbound to firms
approaching SAM expiration or with stale registrations; content/SEO on "SAM renewal
/ 8(a) annual review / HUBZone recertification / SDVOSB recertification"; partnerships
with PTACs/APEX Accelerators, GovCon proposal consultants, and SBA-resource
partners who encounter contractors that need ongoing credential maintenance.
