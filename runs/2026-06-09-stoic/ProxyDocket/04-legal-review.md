LEGAL / REGULATORY REVIEW — ProxyDocket

Not formal legal advice. I am an experienced operator with regulatory pattern-recognition flagging issues the founders must brief real counsel on — not a licensed attorney resolving them.

ONE-PARAGRAPH RISK READ
ProxyDocket sits in an unusually favorable legal corner: unclaimed-property (UP) compliance is governed by state holder statutes (most based on RUUPA 2016) administered by state treasurers/controllers, and preparing/filing those reports is treated as administrative/accounting compliance work — NOT a licensed activity, NOT the practice of law, and NOT a regulated financial-services business like money transmission. The two genuine landmines: (1) the UPL line, which is real but narrow — applying a state's dormancy/exemption rules and preparing NAUPA reports is administrative work Ryan and True Partners already do at scale without law licenses, but rendering written opinions on disputed legal questions drifts toward UPL and CPA-style tax-advice territory; and (2) handling client funds remitted to states plus owner PII in due-diligence mailings, which create custody/escrow exposure and state-privacy exposure that must be engineered around (ideally: never take custody of remittance funds; client wires to the state directly). The dominant residual risk is professional-liability E&O — an error that triggers or worsens a multi-million-dollar audit assessment — which is insurable but requires a tight engagement-letter liability cap. Manageable, not fatal, with two or three structural choices that materially de-risk it.

REGULATORY MAP
- State unclaimed-property statutes (50 states + DC + PR), administered by state treasurers/controllers/revenue. Most modeled on RUUPA (2016) or older Uniform Acts; Delaware operates its own regime (Del. Code Title 12, Ch. 11) and is the dominant audit forum for incorporated holders. The client (holder) bears the statutory reporting/remittance duty; ProxyDocket is the holder's agent/preparer — the single most important risk-reducing fact. ProxyDocket is not a "holder" and not licensed by these regimes.
- UPL — state bar rules / UPL statutes. Applying law to specific facts and rendering legal conclusions can constitute law practice. Market reality: Big-4, Ryan, True Partners, Sovos perform property-identification, dormancy analysis, exemption application, and NAUPA prep as accounting/compliance services without practicing law. The line ProxyDocket must respect: prepare reports, apply published rules mechanically, flag issues; route genuinely disputed legal interpretation and any opinion letter to the client's counsel. Low-to-moderate if scoped correctly — a drafting/positioning problem, not a licensing wall.
- CPA/accounting regulation — state boards. UP compliance is adjacent to tax/accounting but generally NOT "attest" work or CPA-reserved. Avoid "audit"/assurance language. Low if avoided. Founders need not be CPAs (Ryan staffs with non-CPA consultants).
- Custody of client funds / money transmission — state MT acts (NY DFS, CA DFPI), escrow/fiduciary law. IF ProxyDocket receives client money and forwards it to states, it holds funds in trust — escrow/fiduciary duties, commingling risk, a colorable (usually exempt) money-transmission question. HIGH if they take custody; NEAR-ZERO if the client remits directly to the state and ProxyDocket only prepares/files. A structural choice to make before launch.
- State data-privacy (CCPA/CPRA + VA/CO/CT/TX + ~15 more) and GLBA for FI clients. Due-diligence mailings require owner PII (names, addresses, account numbers, sometimes SSNs). ProxyDocket is a service provider/processor on the holder's instructions. Moderate; founders' SOC 2/HIPAA/GDPR experience covers it. Needs DPAs, security controls, SSN care.
- FTC Safeguards Rule / 50-state breach-notification. Bulk owner PII makes a breach a multi-state notification event. Moderate; standard for a B2B processor.
- TCPA/CAN-SPAM/state mail rules for due-diligence outreach. UP due-diligence is statutorily mandated mail (first-class to last known address; some states certified above thresholds) — the safest posture, legally required notices not marketing. Low if outreach stays to required mail.
- Delaware VDA program (DE Secretary of State, distinct from DE escheator audits). VDA representation is recognized non-lawyer consulting. Low-moderate.

LICENSING SUMMARY
No specialized occupational license is required to prepare/file UP reports on a client's behalf — the favorable core fact, demonstrated by the existing market (Ryan, True Partners, Sovos, Big-4 UP groups use non-attorney, frequently non-CPA personnel). Founders need only standard business formation (a properly capitalized LLC/C-corp liability shield) + a local business license. No state UP regulator licenses preparers; no CPA license (work is not attest/assurance); no law license PROVIDED the engagement is scoped as administrative compliance preparation and disputed legal interpretation is referred out. The one place a license-type question genuinely arises is custody of remittance funds — that path could invite money-transmitter/escrow characterization in NY/CA; the clean answer is structural: do not take custody. The named UP specialist is the correct scaffolding for the expertise barrier and adds credibility, not a licensing requirement.

LIABILITY POSTURE
Center of gravity is professional liability/E&O, not regulatory enforcement against the firm. ProxyDocket's work product directly affects whether a client passes/fails a state UP audit; an error (missed property, wrong dormancy period, mis-applied exemption, blown deadline) can produce or enlarge a seven-figure assessment with interest/penalties, and the client will look to ProxyDocket to make it whole. Insurable — professional/E&O + cyber/data-breach — but premium and survivability depend on a well-drafted engagement letter with (a) a liability cap (fees-paid or a fixed multiple), (b) clear allocation that the holder retains the statutory duty and final sign-off, (c) explicit disclaimer that ProxyDocket provides no legal/tax opinions, (d) client responsibility for source-data completeness/accuracy. PII exposure is standard B2B-processor risk, covered by cyber + a DPA; founders' SOC 2/GDPR background means they know how to run this. The custody-of-funds question, if not engineered away, is the one place fiduciary/commingling liability becomes acute — route client funds directly to states. Pre-entity, founders carry personal exposure; solved by forming the entity and signing in its name before the first engagement. Concentrated, insurable, contractually cappable — but genuinely high-stakes per engagement, demanding disciplined engagement letters from day one.

AVERAGE SCORE: 6.2 / 10

TOP 3 LEGAL/REGULATORY STRENGTHS
- No licensing wall: preparing/filing UP reports is administrative/accounting compliance work established players perform without law or CPA licenses, so the founders' lack of credentials is not disqualifying.
- Clean consumer-protection profile: pure B2B with statutorily mandated owner notices means no auto-renewal/ROSCA exposure and minimal TCPA/CAN-SPAM risk.
- Tailwind regulatory trajectory: state UP enforcement is intensifying via contingency auditors and RUUPA adoption, so direction creates rather than erodes demand.

TOP 3 LEGAL/REGULATORY RISKS
- E&O / assessment liability: a preparation error can cause or magnify a seven-figure audit assessment, concentrating high financial exposure in each engagement.
- UPL / advice-line drift: applying dormancy/exemption rules to specific facts, and especially issuing written exemption conclusions, can be characterized as unauthorized practice of law or unlicensed tax advice if not carefully scoped and referred out.
- Funds-custody and PII exposure: handling remittance funds invites escrow/money-transmitter characterization (NY DFS, CA DFPI), and bulk owner PII (SSNs, account numbers) triggers CCPA/CPRA, GLBA, and 50-state breach-notification duties.

BIGGEST SINGLE RISK
E&O liability tied to audit assessments, amplified by the UPL/advice line. Because the client-holder retains the statutory duty but relies entirely on ProxyDocket's property identification, dormancy calculations, and exemption determinations, any material error flows downstream into a state audit — and UP audits are uniquely punishing: multi-state, 10-to-15-year lookbacks, contingency-fee auditors (Kelmar) incentivized to maximize, and statutory authority to estimate liability when records are incomplete, routinely producing seven-figure assessments plus interest and penalties. A client hit with such an assessment will allege ProxyDocket's negligence caused or failed to prevent it. Insurable with E&O and cappable with a well-drafted engagement letter, but structurally high-stakes per client, and the UPL problem makes it worse: if ProxyDocket renders an exemption opinion (rather than mechanically applying published rules and referring disputes to client counsel), it both increases liability and risks a bar UPL complaint. Mitigation is within the founders' control — disciplined engagement letters with liability caps, allocation of the statutory duty to the holder, a no-legal-opinion disclaimer, the named specialist's review documented as administrative not legal, and a referral channel to outside counsel — but must be right from the first engagement, because a single uncapped early deal could threaten the firm.

QUESTIONS FOR REAL COUNSEL BEFORE LAUNCH
1. UPL boundary: where exactly is the line between permissible administrative application of published dormancy/exemption rules and impermissible legal interpretation, and should the engagement letter disclaim legal/tax opinions and route disputes to the client's counsel?
2. Funds custody: if we ever receive client money to remit, do we become an escrow agent/fiduciary/money transmitter under NY (NYDFS), CA (DFPI), or other acts — and does the "agent paying an obligation of the payor" exemption reliably protect us? Should we structurally require clients to remit directly to states?
3. E&O and contractual cap: what liability cap, indemnity, and data-accuracy allocation belong in the engagement letter, and what E&O + cyber limits/premiums are realistic given seven-figure assessment exposure and Kelmar estimation risk?
4. Data privacy/security: as a processor handling owner PII (SSNs, account data) and GLBA-covered FI data, what DPA terms, Safeguards Rule controls, and 50-state breach-notification readiness do we need, and special rules for SSN-bearing mailings?
5. Entity and personal exposure: what entity structure, capitalization, and contracting discipline keep the founders free of personal liability, and must all engagements be signed before work begins?
6. Naming/IP: does the name clear trademark in the relevant classes, and does use of the NAUPA standard / ingestion of published state rules create any licensing/copyright issue?

RECOMMENDATION: GO
The legal/regulatory posture is favorable enough to proceed, with conditions. Decisive facts: this is unlicensed-by-design work the incumbent market already performs without law/CPA credentials, it is clean B2B with almost no consumer-protection exposure, and the regulatory trajectory pushes demand toward the service. The two structural choices that turn "manageable" into "clean," both within the founders' control and to be made before the first engagement: (1) never take custody of remittance funds — require clients to wire directly to states, eliminating the money-transmitter/escrow exposure; (2) scope every engagement as administrative compliance preparation with an explicit no-legal-opinion disclaimer, a fees-based liability cap, holder-retains-the-duty allocation, and a referral channel for disputed legal questions, supported by E&O + cyber insurance. The founders' SOC 2/HIPAA/GDPR experience covers the PII/processor obligations; the named specialist scaffolds the expertise barrier. The residual high-stakes E&O exposure is the normal cost of professional-services work in this niche and is insurable and contractually contained. Retain counsel for the engagement-letter template, the funds-custody/MT analysis, and UPL scoping before the first client; everything else is monitor-and-maintain.
