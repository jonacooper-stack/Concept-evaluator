# SYNTHESIS — ProxyDocket
Date: 2026-06-09
Council members reviewed: CFO, CMO, COO, Legal/Regulatory, CTO

## ONE-PARAGRAPH PM READ
ProxyDocket is the closest thing this run has produced to the CMMC archetype: a 50-state statutory mandate forces a recurring, deadline-driven, already-budgeted buy; the incumbents are a genuinely sleepy split between expensive Big-4/Ryan hourly advisors and dated DIY software (Sovos, UPExchange); and nobody owns the productized, fixed-fee, done-for-you mid-market lane. The expertise moat is real but bridgeable via a named UP specialist plus the founders' compliance-operator background, and the legal posture is unusually clean (unlicensed-by-design, pure B2B, custody risk engineerable away). The honest catch — flagged independently by CMO, COO, and CTO — is that the must-have is acute only for the audit/VDA/IPO minority and latent (vitamin-like) for the cold base, while the true unit of work is bespoke financial-data ingestion plus specialist judgment that resists the "software does it all" framing, threatening both margin and the recurring spine. A strong, structurally-sound concept honestly in REFINE territory, not yet a clean GO, because two load-bearing numbers (ingestion automatability and cleanup-to-subscription conversion) are unproven and sit directly on the income-ladder math.

## WHERE THE EXPERTS AGREE
- Demand is real, mandatory, recurring, already-funded — money flows to Ryan/Big-4 hourly today; not a market-creation play.
- The competitive lane is genuinely open and archetype-fitting (self-service tools vs hourly enterprise advisors; nobody productized in the mid-market).
- The biggest threat is the same across CFO/COO/CTO: per-client data ingestion + specialist judgment may NOT compress into software, turning a 75%-margin SaaS into a ~50% labor-bound consultancy.
- The recurring spine depends on converting project-shaped acute engagements (audit/VDA cleanups) into ongoing subscriptions — unproven.
- Skill/legal fit favorable: no licensing wall; founders' compliance background bridges the moat.

## TOP 5 RISKS (RANKED)
1. Ingestion doesn't automate → margin collapse (CFO/COO/CTO). If <30–40% of AP/AR/payroll/GL lands clean, every client is a manual reconciliation project and blended margin slides ~75%→~50%. Reducer: time 3–5 real extracts before committing build-weeks.
2. Demand bifurcation / recurring-spine failure (CMO/CFO). Acute buyers are project-shaped and finite; the cold base is vitamin-like. Reducer: price so every cleanup lands the client into the recurring subscription; measure conversion on the first 5.
3. E&O / assessment liability amplified by UPL drift (Legal, scores 4 and 3). A preparation error can cause/enlarge a seven-figure Kelmar assessment. Reducer: capped engagement letter, holder-retains-duty allocation, no-legal-opinion disclaimer, E&O + cyber from the first engagement.
4. Oct 31–Nov 1 seasonality spike caps leanness at scale (COO). ~1.5 FTE in 8 weeks at 55–65 clients; a botched fall cycle destroys trust. Reducer: pre-design seasonal-analyst + second-specialist + filing-sequence staggering.
5. 50-state rules-engine maintenance treadmill + key-person specialist (CTO dim 10=4, COO). Staleness causes client penalties. Reducer: license maintained NAUPA content (Sovos/Eagle); contract the specialist with an SLA + named backup.

## TOP 3 STRENGTHS
- Archetype-fit demand: a 50-state mandate forcing a recurring, penalized, already-budgeted buy, with Kelmar as a named dreaded enforcement engine (strongest-evidenced dimension).
- A genuine, founder-prized competitive gap with a bridgeable moat (productized fixed-fee mid-market service between self-service tools and hourly advisors; no licensing wall).
- Favorable financial/legal shape: upfront-annual billing self-funds growth, low five-figure launch, clean B2B, a named strategic-buyer exit comp (Sovos).

## THE BIGGEST OPEN QUESTION
Does the software meaningfully compress the work, or is this a consulting practice in a software costume? It hinges on one measurable: across real client extracts, what fraction of ingestion lands clean on first pass, and how many specialist-hours per account — sublinear or linear with revenue? Answerable in 1–2 weeks with 3–5 real extracts + a timed test filing, and it should be answered before any build commitment.
