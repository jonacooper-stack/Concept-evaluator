# FranAudit — Clean-Room One-Pager

## What it is
A productized, recurring managed service that keeps a franchisor's Franchise
Disclosure Document (FDD) current and legally fileable, and handles the annual
renewal and multi-state franchise registrations the franchisor must complete to
keep selling franchises. Software runs the deadline calendar, document assembly,
multi-state filing workflow, and audited-financials coordination; a partnered
franchise attorney provides the legal review/sign-off on the disclosure content.

## The customer
Emerging and small-to-mid US franchisors — brands with roughly 1 to 100 franchised
units — especially newer franchisors who have outgrown DIY but cannot absorb a
large franchise law firm's annual fees. These owners must maintain an FDD and
register/renew in the states that require it, but have no in-house franchise-legal
or compliance staff.

## The pain
Under the FTC Franchise Rule (16 CFR Part 436), a franchisor must furnish a current
FDD to prospective franchisees; the FDD must be updated annually within 120 days of
the franchisor's fiscal year-end (and amended for material changes), and it must
include audited financial statements. Separately, ~13 "registration states"
(including CA, NY, IL, MD, VA, WA, MN, WI) require the franchisor to file and obtain
registration/renewal before lawfully offering or selling franchises there, and a
handful more are "filing/notice" states. The consequences are revenue-gating: a
stale, unfiled, or lapsed FDD/registration means the franchisor legally cannot sell
franchises in that state, deals stall, and state franchise examiners issue comment
letters that must be cleared. The deadline is hard, annual, and public, and the work
recurs every year plus on every material change.

## The product / service
1. **Annual FDD renewal** — update all 23 FDD items, coordinate the franchisor's
   CPA-provided audited financials, refresh the franchisee/outlet tables and
   litigation/financial-performance disclosures, and produce the filing-ready FDD.
2. **Multi-state registration & renewal filing** — prepare and submit each
   registration/renewal/notice filing, track every state's status and fees, and
   manage examiner comment-letter responses.
3. **Deadline & compliance calendar** — a tracked calendar of the 120-day renewal
   deadline, each state's renewal date, and material-change amendment triggers.
4. **Attorney sign-off** — a partnered franchise attorney reviews and signs off on
   the legal disclosure content; the service handles assembly, filing, tracking,
   and project management around that review.

## Revenue model
Recurring annual subscription covering the renewal cycle plus a number of state
filings, tiered by number of registration states and unit/disclosure complexity:
[ASSUMED] ~$8,000–$30,000/yr (vs. a large franchise firm often charging $20K–$50K+
for the same annual cycle on an hourly basis), with add-on fees for extra-state
filings and mid-year material-change amendments. Recurring because the renewal and
multi-state registrations are mandatory every year.

## Positioning
"Your FDD, renewed and filed in every state, on time, every year — for a fixed
annual fee, not a law-firm hourly bill. You keep selling franchises; we keep you
compliant and the examiners happy."

## Competitive landscape (stated neutrally)
The space is served by franchise law firms (the dominant providers — they own FDD
drafting because it is legal work, but they are expensive, hourly, and
transactional), franchise-development consultancies and FDD-prep services (many of
which already partner with attorneys), and franchise-data/registration tools
(e.g., FRANdata-style data services). There is no dominant productized,
software-leveraged, fixed-fee recurring managed offering aimed at the small/emerging
franchisor; the incumbents are either expensive law firms or fragmented consultants.

## Why these founders
- Marketing/positioning to reach a fragmented, growing base of emerging franchisors
  who feel gouged by hourly legal bills.
- Software to build the deadline calendar, FDD-assembly, and multi-state
  filing-workflow engine.
- Compliance-operator instinct to run a deadline-and-document program.
- The franchise-law expertise is a real but bridgeable moat: the FDD format and
  state registration requirements are codified, and a partnered/named franchise
  attorney provides the legal sign-off that keeps the work defensible and keeps
  non-specialist competitors out.

## Acquisition motion
Buyers are enumerable from the public state franchise registries (every registered
franchisor with its renewal date is on file in CA DFPI, NY, IL, MN, WI, etc.) and
from FDD databases; outbound to franchisors approaching their renewal window;
content/SEO on "FDD renewal deadline / franchise registration"; partnerships with
franchise brokers, franchise-development consultants, and the CPAs who produce the
audited financials (a natural referral channel).
