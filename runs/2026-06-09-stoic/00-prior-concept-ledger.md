## Recurring failure modes (why everything clustered 6.3–7.4) — AVOID THESE
1. **Unvalidated demand.** Market-pull (weight 20, the heaviest) was repeatedly the
   lowest dimension — "reasoned, not evidenced." Favor niches where the trigger is
   a **public, scrapeable deadline/registry/filing** and the buyer is enumerable.
2. **Catastrophic-liability coupling.** "Never lose your X" promises sit one causal
   step from a 7-figure customer disaster → Legal/QC capped at ~5, uninsurable E&O
   tail on a low-five-figure fee. Favor mandates where a service error is NOT
   directly causal to a catastrophic loss.
3. **Scarce-specialist bottleneck / no inbound channel.** Judgment-heavy delivery
   (credentialed reviewer) caps margin + growth; no-search categories force slow
   relationship GTM. Favor a **deeper labor pool + a findable buyer**.
4. **Software-vs-BPO margin hinge.** If the exception/portal/filing work doesn't
   compress with software, it's a labor-bound BPO, not a productized service.
5. **Thin defensibility.** Bootstrapped productized services are copyable; network/
   system-of-record moats kept dying on cold-start or a funded incumbent owning the rail.
6. **Funded-incumbent encroachment (constraint #5).** Head-on entry into categories
   owned by well-funded strong-tech players (Anrok, Vanta, Drata, AgentSync, Veeva,
   NGOsource, Exostar) is an automatic drag/DQ.

## Design implications for THIS run's new candidates
Hunt for: a forced/recurring buy with a **public, enumerable, scrapeable trigger**;
**low catastrophic-liability coupling**; a **deep (not scarce) labor pool**; work
that genuinely **compresses with software**; **sleepy non-tech incumbents** and no
funded strong-tech player; a **findable inbound channel**; capital-light, remote,
NOT field-ops. Avoid every theme in the table above.
