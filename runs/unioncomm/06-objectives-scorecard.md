IDEA: Member Engagement & Dues-Collection Overlay for Labor Unions
ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION: A usage/take-rate-priced software overlay on a union's legacy system of record that re-signs members, captures missing personal contact data, and collects dues digitally (card/ACH) outside employer payroll, with hierarchical email/SMS comms.

HARD CONSTRAINT CHECK
- Buildable w/ founder skills, not field-ops core? [Y — pure software + payments/SMS rails; zero field ops; squarely software/marketing/compliance, all founder strengths. COO logistics scored 9.]
- Low capital required? [Y — low five figures + founder dev time; SOC 2 ~$15–40K is the main real outlay. CFO startup-capital 8.]
- Recurring revenue component? [Y — take-rate on recurring monthly/annual dues + recurring SMS usage; >90% repeating once dues flow through the rail.]
- Plausible ~$300K/founder by ~24mo? [Y, but contingent — CFO pencils ~$1M combined GP at ~125K transacting members via a few large-affiliate wins; reachable but NOT conservative, gated by digital-dues adoption per member.]
- Incumbents stale/sleepy (not strong-tech, well-funded)? [MIXED — records incumbents (UnionWare, Union Link) are genuinely sleepy and beatable; BUT the comms half is contested by Bonterra/EveryAction/ActionNetwork, which are not sleepy and already process payments. Not disqualifying, but not a clean "yes."]
- No specialized credentials required? [Y — no occupational license; founders' SOC 2/HIPAA/GDPR background covers the compliance surface. Labor-world relationships are missing but learnable/bridgeable.]
> No hard "N". Not disqualified. Advances to scoring.

DIMENSION SCORES (1–10)
1. Market pull / must-have (w20):        8 — Post-Janus (2018) + state law made direct dues collection an existential, budgeted, urgent problem unions are demonstrably losing (SEIU shed ~210K agency-fee payers); a true painkiller. Held off 9 because the BUY is slow, political, consensus-gated — not "bought without internal selling."
2. Recurring revenue strength (w15):     8 — Take-rate on recurring dues + recurring SMS usage; >90% repeating and sticky once the union's money and fresh contact data flow through the rail. Held off 9 by thin per-unit economics and rate-compression risk.
3. Competitive landscape (w15):          6 — Records incumbents (UnionWare, Union Link) are sleepy and beatable, but the comms/payments seam is contested by non-sleepy Bonterra/ActionNetwork that already do donation processing and could extend into dues collection in <12 months; defensibility is a feature bundle, not structural.
4. Path to founder income (w15):         6 — CFO math reaches the ladder (~$1M combined GP by mo 24) but needs ~125K members actually transacting dues — credible on a few large-affiliate wins, contingent on a digital-dues adoption rate the platform does not control. Not conservative.
5. Probability of success (w10):         5 — Multiple compounding gates: founders lack labor-world relationships, union federalism may block top-down mandate, take-rate must survive a finance committee, adoption must convert off payroll. Demand is proven; execution path is uncertain.
6. Capital efficiency (w10):             8 — Launchable for low five figures + founder dev time; cash-flow positive fast; take-rate skimmed inside the transaction means near-zero working capital (CFO working-capital 9).
7. Operational tractability (w5):        7 — Remote-first, 50-state, no field ops (COO logistics 9), but a real payments-operations tax (ACH returns, chargebacks, reconciliation) and bespoke legacy-DB onboarding cap it below excellent.
8. Scalability optionality (w5):         7 — Horizontal expansion to associations, advocacy groups, nonprofits is plausible; a take-rate-on-dues asset with embedded payments is acquirable/transferable (CFO financeability 7).
9. Skill fit (versatility) (w5):         7 — Squarely software/marketing/positioning/GTM/finance/compliance — all founder strengths; only gap is labor-movement relationships, which is learnable/bridgeable, not a credential wall.

TOP 3 STRENGTHS
- Genuine, dated, existential market pull (post-Janus dues collection) — a real painkiller, not a vitamin.
- Capital-light, recurring, high-margin, no-field-ops model squarely in the founders' skill set.
- Sleepy records incumbents (UnionWare, Union Link) on the system-of-record side leave a real seam.

TOP 3 RISKS
- Thin per-member take-rate that is behaviorally gated by adoption the platform doesn't control, and exposed to rate compression by a sophisticated buyer.
- Contested, copyable comms/payments seam (Bonterra/ActionNetwork already process money and own the comms relationship).
- Relationship-gated, politically slow buyer where the founders have no existing standing and a dues take-rate must survive a finance-committee/floor-vote objection.
