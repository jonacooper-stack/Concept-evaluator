# Concept One-Pager — Member Engagement & Dues-Collection Overlay for Labor Unions

## Customer
Labor unions in the United States — national and state-level union bodies and
their subordinate district and local chapters (e.g., public-sector unions like
teachers, state employees, AFSCME/SEIU/NEA-style affiliates, and trade unions).
The economic buyer is national or state union leadership; the deployed users are
chapter officers and staff down to the local level. A meaningful adjacent market
is other dues-based membership organizations: professional associations,
advocacy groups, and non-profits.

## The pain (why this is urgent now)
Many unions, especially public-sector ones, have lost the ability to collect
dues automatically through employer payroll deduction. Following the 2018 *Janus
v. AFSCME* Supreme Court decision and a wave of state-level legislation, public
unions can no longer assume members are auto-enrolled or that dues flow through
the government employer's payroll system. Unions must now affirmatively re-sign
members, capture personal (non-employer) contact information, and collect dues
directly. Most unions discover their database of record is missing members' cell
phone numbers and personal emails, and they have no fast digital way to re-enroll
members or collect money outside payroll. This is an existential revenue problem
for the union, not a nice-to-have.

## The product
A lightweight software overlay that sits on top of — and integrates with — the
union's existing legacy membership database/system of record rather than
replacing it. It provides:
- Hierarchical, multi-level communication (national → state → district → local)
  over email and SMS text, with permissioned roles per tier.
- High-conversion digital onboarding/renewal workflows: structured digital
  renewal links that re-sign members and capture missing contact data (cell
  phone, personal email).
- Direct-to-member digital dues collection (credit card and ACH direct debit)
  that bypasses the employer payroll system entirely.

## Revenue model
No flat per-seat SaaS license. A free entry tier (basic email/communication) is
used to get leadership to mandate and roll the tool down to every chapter,
removing procurement and budget friction. Money is made on usage and
transactions:
- A markup on SMS message volume sent through the platform.
- A payment-processing take-rate on every dollar of dues collected digitally via
  card or ACH.
Revenue therefore scales with member volume, message volume, and dues throughput
rather than with seat count.

## Positioning
Against incumbent membership-management systems and databases of record — most
prominently UnionWare and Union Link — which are heavy, desktop-first systems
built for compliance, grievance tracking, and records, and which treat real-time
member communication and digital money movement as secondary. The overlay is
positioned as the fast, modern engagement-and-collections layer that deploys in
days across chapters without ripping out the institutional database underneath,
priced on usage instead of long seat-licensed enterprise contracts.
