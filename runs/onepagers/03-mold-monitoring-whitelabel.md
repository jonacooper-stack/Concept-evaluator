# Clean-Room One-Pager — White-Label Moisture & Mold Monitoring for Restoration Firms

## The customer
The paying customer is the water-damage restoration and basement-waterproofing contractor —
the firms homeowners call after a flood, a burst pipe, a wet basement, or a mold scare. The
U.S. has tens of thousands of these contractors (independent shops and franchise locations).
They already go into homes, already have a customer relationship and a service truck, and are
always looking for a recurring-revenue add-on to a business that is otherwise one-and-done
project work. The end-beneficiary is that contractor's homeowner client, but the contractor is
the buyer, the brand on the product, and the channel.

## The problem
Two linked problems. For the homeowner: after a remediation or waterproofing job, moisture
problems silently recur — a basement re-wets, humidity creeps back, and mold regrows behind
the wall — and nobody knows until there is visible damage and another expensive emergency.
For the contractor: their revenue is lumpy, one-time, and project-based; once the job is done
the customer relationship goes cold, there is no recurring income, and when moisture does
recur the homeowner may blame the original work or call a competitor. The contractor has no
easy, branded way to stay in the home, prove their work is holding, and earn ongoing revenue.

## The product / service
A white-labeled moisture/humidity monitoring service the contractor sells to their homeowner
clients under the contractor's own brand:
- Low-cost wireless sensors placed in at-risk areas (basement, crawlspace, behind problem
  walls) that continuously read humidity and moisture and phone home over wifi/cellular.
- A monitoring backend that watches the readings and raises an alert (to the contractor and/or
  homeowner) when moisture trends toward a danger threshold — early warning before damage.
- A branded homeowner app/dashboard and a monthly "your home is dry / here's a reading" report,
  all carrying the contractor's logo, not ours.
- A contractor-side dashboard showing all their monitored homes, alerts, and which accounts are
  due for a service visit — turning alerts into scheduled, billable follow-up work.

We provide the hardware, the software, the alerting, and the branding kit; the contractor
provides the customer, the install (often bundled into the job they're already doing), and the
trusted local relationship.

## The revenue model
Recurring B2B subscription billed to the contractor, per monitored home, roughly $10–$40 per
home per month (the contractor marks it up and charges the homeowner more, keeping the spread,
or bundles it into a service plan). A contractor with a few hundred past customers can put
dozens to hundreds of homes under monitoring over time. Revenue is recurring, seat-like (per
home), and expands automatically as the contractor adds each new job to the program. An
optional hardware fee or modest activation charge covers sensor cost; the monitoring
subscription is the durable spine.

## Positioning
"Give your customers a reason to keep paying you — your own branded 'is-my-home-still-dry'
monitoring service, with none of the software to build." Positioned to the contractor against
the status quo of zero recurring revenue and cold post-job relationships, and against generic
consumer smart-home sensors (which carry no contractor brand, generate no contractor revenue,
and create no service-visit pipeline). The wedge is that we are not selling monitoring to
homeowners — we are selling a turnkey recurring-revenue product line to contractors who already
own the customer.

## How it's delivered and grown
The hardware is off-the-shelf or lightly customized wireless humidity/moisture sensors; the
software is standard cloud IoT ingestion plus a branded web/app layer and an alerting engine.
The business is grown by signing restoration/waterproofing contractors and franchise groups —
each signed contractor becomes a self-expanding channel that adds homes over time — through
restoration-industry trade associations, franchise networks, industry trade shows, and direct
outreach to contractor owners. The founders handle contractor sales/partnerships, the branded-
product packaging, and the software/monitoring layer.
