COO REVIEW — Fractional Data & Analytics Leader for Mid-Market Companies

ONE-PARAGRAPH OPERATIONS READ
This is a high-touch professional-services retainer where the deliverable is substantially the two founders' own senior judgment — exactly the kind of business where the operational ceiling is not demand but the number of hours two experienced people have in a week. The tooling and dashboard build are genuinely tractable (off-the-shelf cloud warehouse, managed connectors, BI layer), so the operational risk is not "can we build it" — it is "can two senior advisors carry enough simultaneous retainers, at a high enough monthly price, to clear roughly $2M each ($4M combined distributable) within 24 months, while the most valuable, least-delegable part of the work is their own face time in monthly executive reviews and ad-hoc CEO questions." Delivery is repeatable in its scaffolding but bespoke in its judgment, and the leverage model (junior/contract analysts extending each account) helps the build hours but barely touches the advisory hours the buyer is actually paying for. The honest read: very operable as a strong lifestyle consultancy at $0.7M–$1.5M combined; structurally strained to reach $4M combined on two founders' calendars within 24 months without either a pricing level the mid-market resists or a headcount build the one-pager only gestures at.

THE TUESDAY-IN-MARCH WALKTHROUGH
Picture month 12. Say the founders have 9 active retainers at avg $8K/month ($72K MRR; ~$864K annualized — well short of $4M but realistic for two people selling and delivering simultaneously). On this Tuesday in March:
- Founder A preps at 8:00 for a 9:00 monthly executive review with a $60M distribution client's CEO and CFO — the marquee deliverable. The dashboards refreshed overnight, but two numbers don't tie — the ERP pushed credit memos that double-counted in the revenue view. Founder A reconciles by hand 8:00–8:45 and adds a caveat slide. Review runs 9:00–10:15. The CFO asks an unplanned question ("which top-20 customers are below blended margin after freight?") — now an unbudgeted 3–4 hours of analyst-plus-advisor work this week.
- 10:30–12:00: Founder A reviews a contract analyst's work (one offshore analyst ~$2.5K/month building/maintaining pipelines across 4 of 9 accounts). The analyst mis-mapped store IDs on a new POS connector; Founder A catches it, corrects, re-runs — the leverage tax: the analyst extends build capacity but cannot face a CEO, and every output passes under a founder's eye.
- Founder B: 8:30–10:00 a discovery/scoping call with a $90M logistics prospect (fractional-CFO referral); 10:30–12:00 the system-wiring sprint for a 3-week-old client (QuickBooks + ADP + homegrown CRM into the warehouse) — front-loaded, least-recurring work competing directly with selling and delivering.
- Afternoon: Founder A does the Thursday margin analysis (3 hrs), a 30-min "dashboard looks wrong" call (a returns-vs-revenue definitional dispute — a recurring friction), 45 min of ad-hoc Slack questions. Founder B writes the logistics proposal and a 45-min referral-partner call.
Where it breaks: the monthly reviews and ad-hoc CEO questions are the product and are non-delegable founder time. With 9 clients each expecting ~75-min monthly review + ~3–6 hrs between-meeting analysis + onboarding sprints + the entire sales motion on the same two calendars, the founders are near a comfortable ceiling at well under half the income target. The contract analyst helps the pipelines, not the advisory load — and the advisory load justifies the $8K price.

CAPACITY MATH (show the work)
Target: ~$2M/founder = ~$4M combined distributable in 24 months. Services margin ~55–65% after analyst/tooling/overhead [ASSUMED]. To net ~$4M distributable, revenue ~$6M–$7M/year.
Revenue/client: midpoint $8K/mo = $96K/yr; top $12K/mo = $144K/yr.
Clients required: at $8K avg, $6.5M/$96K ≈ 68 retainers; at $12K avg (optimistic for mid-market), $6.5M/$144K ≈ 45 retainers.
Founder-time ceiling — non-delegable per client per month: monthly review ~2.5 hrs (1.25 in-meeting + 1.25 prep); ad-hoc + relationship + analyst oversight ~4–6 hrs [ASSUMED]. Total ~6.5–8.5 hrs/client/month; call it 7.
Two founders' advisory capacity: ~25 hrs/week each client-facing = ~430 hrs/month combined.
Max on advisory time alone: 430/7 ≈ 61 clients IF they did nothing but deliver. Realistic mix (~40% to sales/onboarding/oversight) leaves ~258 advisory hrs/month → 258/7 ≈ 37 clients as a practical steady-state ceiling for two people personally owning every account.
Collision: $4M combined needs ~45–68 retainers; two founders top out ~35–40 before quality breaks. To bridge: (a) push price toward/above $12K (mid-market resistance — these $10-150M unglamorous firms balk at a $250K hire, so $144K/year is a hard sell), or (b) genuinely delegate the advisory layer — hire senior "fractional data leaders" who are NOT the founders, turning a two-person practice into a staffed firm with recruiting/training/QC overhead the one-pager doesn't price.
Onboarding is a second bottleneck: ~40–80 founder/analyst hours per onboarding [ASSUMED]; reaching 45+ clients in 24 months means ~2 net-new/month sustained, each competing with delivery and sales for the same hours.
Conclusion: reaches a strong lifestyle outcome (~$1.5–2.5M combined revenue, ~$0.8–1.4M combined distributable) comfortably; reaching $4M combined distributable in 24 months requires building a staffed senior-advisory team — a different, harder problem than "two founders plus contract analysts."

AVERAGE SCORE: 5.9 / 10

TOP 3 OPERATIONAL STRENGTHS
- Near-zero physical logistics and a fully off-the-shelf, multi-sourced tooling stack — no fleet/inventory/MOQs/install windows; delivery is a 75-minute call plus dashboards refreshing on managed pipelines.
- Genuinely recurring, sticky delivery shape: once dashboards are embedded in the monthly business review, the cadence renews indefinitely; demand is year-round and weather-neutral.
- Real, if partial, leverage path: cheap contract/offshore analysts (~$2–4K/month) can absorb build-and-maintain hours, freeing founder time for the high-value advisory layer.

TOP 3 OPERATIONAL RISKS
- The deliverable is the founders' own non-delegable hours; the income target collides with a two-person calendar ceiling (~35–40 personally-owned retainers vs ~45–68 needed for $4M distributable).
- The "cheap analyst" lever relieves build work but not advisory work — the part the client actually pays $8–12K/month for — so it does not break the real bottleneck.
- QC rests on a founder manually catching mis-mapped, non-tying data across heterogeneous client systems; high-variance, doesn't standardize, and the failure mode (a wrong number in front of a CEO) directly damages the trust the retainer depends on.

BIGGEST SINGLE RISK
The single operational issue most likely to break this is the collision between the income target and the founder-time ceiling. The product the client buys is a trusted senior advisor personally owning "the numbers" — monthly executive reviews and ad-hoc CEO access — and that is precisely the work that cannot be handed to a $3K/month offshore analyst. My capacity math says two founders, even delivering hard, can personally carry only ~35–40 retainers before review quality and responsiveness degrade, which tops out near $3.4–4.6M of revenue and, after delivery and overhead cost, roughly $1.8–2.8M of combined distributable income — short of the $4M combined ($2M each) goal, and that is the optimistic end assuming high client counts at a price the mid-market resists. The only true fixes both convert the business into something harder than the pitch: raise average price toward/above $12K/month (resisted by exactly the cost-sensitive owner-operators the concept targets), or hire and train senior advisors who are not the founders (recruiting a scarce, CEO-trustworthy profile, plus training/QC and a margin structure the one-pager never costs). I am not sure two people can personally do enough of this work to reach $2M each within 24 months; I am fairly sure they can build a very good $0.8–1.4M-distributable lifestyle practice.
