ONE-PARAGRAPH PM READ
A genuinely smart business-model insight (sell recurring revenue to a contractor desperate to
escape one-and-done project work) bolted onto a hardware/IoT delivery model that fights the
founders on almost every operational and technical axis. Four of five experts land REFINE; only
Legal says GO, and Legal is scoring "low regulatory burden," not "good business." The recurring-
revenue spine the thesis rests on is also the least-proven, least-controllable part: a two-stage
B2B2C sale where the founders own neither the install, the homeowner relationship, nor the price,
sitting on a battery-powered sensor fleet in the worst RF environment in the house that fails
silently and lands liability-grade false-negatives on the contractor's brand. Steady-state
economics are fine; the 24-month path and the permanent hardware-ops burden are where it breaks.
