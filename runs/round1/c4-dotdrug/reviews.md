## CMO (avg 6.7, REFINE)
Positioning 8, **Differentiation 5**, Wedge 8, GTM fit 8, Distribution 7, **CAC 6**, Brand 7, **Competitive air 6**, **Defensibility 6**, **Customer truth 6**.
Wedge strong (free FMCSA new-entrant/SAFER lists). BUT named competent incumbents already run this exact productized model: **Foley Services** (direct), **J.J. ## COO (avg 7.2, GO)

## Legal (avg 6.5, GO)
**Industry burden 4**, Licensing 9, **Data privacy 4**, Employment 7, Consumer protection 8, IP 7, Contract 6, **Liability 5**, Jurisdictional 7, Trajectory 8.
No C/TPA license needed (49 CFR Part 40 defines but doesn't license); federally preemptive (49 USC 31306). BUT heavily regulated domain (burden 4); holds named individuals' drug-test results under Part 40 Subpart P confidentiality + state CMIA → data-privacy 4. Biggest risk: E&O on compliance execution; tail = impaired employee stays on duty → accident → C/TPA named in litigation.
