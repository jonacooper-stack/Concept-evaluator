## Legal (avg 7.5, REC GO)

Main risk: E&O when a deadline missed → customer violation; insurable/cappable. Watch practice-of-engineering line (state PE boards). Tailwind: 2024 LCRI (40 CFR 141 Subpart I), decade-long mandate.
