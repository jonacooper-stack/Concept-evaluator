# Concept One-Pager

## What it is
A productized, recurring managed-compliance service that keeps small and rural
public water systems continuously compliant with the EPA's Lead and Copper Rule
(and its 2024 Lead and Copper Rule Improvements) — building and maintaining their
lead-service-line inventory, managing their sampling schedules and result
filings, drafting and sending the mandatory public notifications, and keeping
all the state primacy-agency paperwork current and on-deadline — so the utility
never misses a filing and never takes an enforcement hit.

## The customer
Small community water systems and very small/rural systems (serving roughly
500–10,000 people): rural water districts, small-town municipal utilities,
mobile-home-park and homeowner-association water systems, and small private
water companies. There are tens of thousands of these in the US. They are run by
one or two overworked operators who also fix pumps and read meters; they have no
dedicated compliance staff, and they are now buried under the Lead and Copper
Rule Improvements (LCRI), which require a baseline lead-service-line inventory,
ongoing inventory updates, service-line replacement tracking, revised tap-sampling
plans, and expanded public-education/notification obligations on hard federal
deadlines.

## The pain (why it's a must-have)
- The LCRI sets a compliance deadline (initial inventory was due October 2024,
  with replacement programs and validation continuing for years) and the
  obligations recur every monitoring period — this is not a one-time project.
- A missed sampling window, a late public notice, or an incomplete inventory is a
  reportable violation that the state primacy agency and EPA can act on, with
  civil penalties and public "violation" listings that embarrass elected boards.
- The work is paperwork- and deadline-heavy and the operators are not equipped
  for it — they entered the trade to run water infrastructure, not to manage a
  recurring federal documentation regime.
- The buy is budgeted: water systems already carry line items for regulatory
  compliance and engineering support, and rate revenue funds it.

## The product
A fixed-scope, published-price recurring service on one delivery spine:
1. Onboarding: build/clean the lead-service-line inventory from the utility's
   existing records (tap cards, as-builts, meter data the utility provides) into
   a structured, state-submittable format; set up the sampling calendar.
2. A compliance system-of-record portal holding the inventory, sampling
   schedule, lab results (entered/uploaded — the certified lab does the actual
   water sampling and analysis), filings, and notification history.
3. Recurring managed cadence: deadline tracking and reminders, preparation and
   submission of periodic inventory updates and monitoring reports to the state
   primacy agency, drafting and issuing the required public-education and
   notification materials, and replacement-program record tracking.
4. Audit/enforcement support: assembling the documentation package when the
   state sanitary survey or EPA inquiry arrives.
The software does the deadline tracking, record-keeping, templated notice
generation, and report assembly; humans handle the judgment, the
primacy-agency relationship, and the board-facing reassurance. No field work —
the utility's own operators and certified labs do all physical sampling.

## Revenue model
Recurring subscription per water system, tiered by population served and
service-line count. Indicative pricing: a one-time onboarding/inventory-build fee
in the low four figures, then an annual subscription in the very-low-to-mid four
figures per system per year, with per-cycle add-ons for monitoring periods that
require heavier filing. Billed annually in advance. Revenue is highly recurring
because the obligation is perpetual and the system-of-record creates switching
cost.

## Positioning
"Never miss a Lead and Copper deadline again. We keep your inventory, your
sampling, your filings, and your public notices done and on time — for one
predictable annual fee — so your board never sees a violation letter." Sold
against sleepy regional engineering firms that bill hourly, treat compliance as a
side product of big capital-project work, and only show up at deadline crunch.

## Go-to-market
Marketing/positioning-led demand generation aimed at small-system operators and
boards (content on LCRI deadlines and penalties, presence at state rural-water
association conferences and AWWA section meetings, targeted outbound to systems
appearing on state non-compliance and inventory-overdue lists), with founder-led
close. State rural water associations and primacy agencies are referral channels
because they want their small systems compliant.

## Who runs it
Two generalist founders strong in marketing/positioning, software, and running
formal compliance programs, supported by a named drinking-water-compliance
practitioner advisor (a former state primacy-program staffer or water-system
compliance specialist) for regulatory judgment. Remote-first, no field
operations.
