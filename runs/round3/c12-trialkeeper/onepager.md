# Concept One-Pager

## What it is
A recurring managed service plus shared site-qualification network that keeps
independent clinical-research sites continuously "study-ready" for the sponsors
and CROs that send them trials — maintaining each site's regulatory and
operational qualification package (site facility and staff credentials, training
records like GCP and protocol-specific certifications, SOPs, regulatory-document
binder readiness, prior-study performance) and the recurring site-qualification
questionnaires and feasibility surveys that every sponsor/CRO sends before
awarding a study — and letting a site assemble that package ONCE and reuse it to
respond to every sponsor's qualification request instead of rebuilding it from
scratch for each new trial.

## The customer
Independent and small-network clinical-research sites — physician-owned research
practices, dedicated research sites, and small site-management organizations
(SMOs) — that run sponsor/CRO trials as a meaningful revenue line, typically
$1M–$30M in research revenue with 2–30 study staff. The buyer is the site owner,
the clinical-operations director, or the regulatory/start-up manager. To win and
keep studies they must repeatedly pass sponsor/CRO site qualification: complete
feasibility and qualification questionnaires, prove staff training and credential
currency, show SOPs and regulatory readiness, and re-verify all of it for every
new sponsor and often every new study — a continuous, deadline-driven overhead
that directly gates whether they are awarded the trial.

## The pain (why it's a must-have)
- Sponsors and CROs REQUIRE site qualification before awarding a study: a
  feasibility/qualification questionnaire, proof of current GCP and
  protocol-specific training, staff CVs and credentials, SOPs, and regulatory
  readiness — and they re-verify it for each new study and each new sponsor.
- The pain is ACUTE and revenue-gating: a slow or incomplete qualification
  response means the site loses the study to a faster site, and study awards are
  the site's revenue — the owner feels a lost trial immediately.
- It RECURS continuously: training and credentials expire and must be refreshed,
  every new sponsor sends its own questionnaire, and a busy site fields dozens of
  qualification/feasibility requests a year, each asking substantially the same
  things in a different format.
- It is duplicative across sponsors: the same site answers the same
  staff-credential and SOP questions for sponsor after sponsor, rebuilding the
  same package every time.

## The product
A fixed-scope recurring service on one delivery spine, software-led:
1. Onboarding: build the site's structured qualification system-of-record — staff
   roster with training/credential currency, CVs, SOP library, facility and
   equipment records, regulatory-readiness checklist, and prior-study performance
   — into one verified, reusable site-qualification package.
2. The shared site-qualification network (the moat): each site's verified package
   becomes a reusable profile, and because sponsors/CROs ask substantially the
   same qualification questions, the system answers each new sponsor's
   questionnaire fast from the site's maintained package and from an accumulating
   library of how the recurring sponsor/CRO questions map to the site's record —
   so the site qualifies once and re-presents many times. Coverage and speed
   compound as more sites and more sponsor questionnaires flow through.
3. Recurring managed cadence: keep staff training/credential currency and the SOP
   and regulatory-readiness records up to date, turn around each incoming
   sponsor/CRO qualification and feasibility questionnaire on a fast SLA, and flag
   expiring credentials before they cost the site a study.
4. Audit/inspection-readiness support: keep the site's regulatory binder and
   training records inspection-ready for sponsor audits.
The software runs the qualification system-of-record, the credential-expiry
tracking, the questionnaire-response engine, and the reusable-profile matching;
humans handle the judgment and the sponsor-facing responses. The site owns and
attests to its own data and remains responsible for actual trial conduct; this
service maintains and re-presents the qualification evidence. No patient data and
no clinical-trial conduct — strictly the site's own credential, training, SOP,
and qualification records. No field operations.

## Revenue model
Recurring subscription per site, scaled by study-staff count and the number of
active sponsor/CRO relationships (mid four to low five figures per year), billed
annually or monthly in advance, with per-heavy-questionnaire overage for
high-volume sites. Revenue is structurally recurring because credentials expire
and must be refreshed, qualification questionnaires arrive continuously, and every
new sponsor relationship reuses the maintained package.

## Positioning
"Win more studies. Stop rebuilding your site-qualification package for every
sponsor — we keep your staff credentials, training, SOPs, and regulatory
readiness current and turn around every sponsor's qualification questionnaire in
days, so you're always the site that's ready first." Sold against the site's
overworked regulatory/start-up coordinator doing it in binders and spreadsheets,
generalist clinical-research consultants billing hourly, and the do-nothing
default that loses studies to faster sites.

## Go-to-market
Marketing/positioning-led demand generation into research-site owners and
clinical-ops/regulatory managers (content on site qualification, feasibility
response, GCP/credential currency, and inspection readiness; targeted outbound to
independent research sites; presence at site-focused clinical-research
conferences and site-network/association events), plus founder-led close, plus
partnerships with the site networks, site-enablement vendors, and
research-coordinator training providers that serve the same sites. As more sites
join, the recurring-questionnaire library deepens and response speed compounds.

## Who runs it
Two generalist founders strong in marketing/positioning, software, and operating
formal compliance/quality programs, supported by a named clinical-research
regulatory/quality practitioner (a former site regulatory manager or GCP/quality
specialist) for the qualification judgment. Remote-first, no field operations.
