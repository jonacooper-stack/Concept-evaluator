# Concept One-Pager

## What it is
A recurring managed service plus shared supplier-qualification network that keeps
small aerospace and defense parts manufacturers continuously "approved-supplier
ready" for the primes and Tier-1s they sell to — maintaining their AS9100 quality
records, First Article Inspection (FAI / AS9102) packages, counterfeit-parts
prevention (AS5553) and conflict-minerals documentation, supplier corrective-
action responses (SCARs), and the recurring re-qualification evidence each
customer demands — and, critically, letting a supplier qualify ONCE and reuse
that verified evidence package across every prime and Tier-1 that asks, instead
of rebuilding it from scratch for each one.

## The customer
Privately-owned small/mid aerospace and defense machine shops, fabricators, and
specialty-process suppliers (heat-treat, plating, NDT-adjacent, electronics
assembly) — typically $3M–$60M revenue, sitting as Tier-2/Tier-3 suppliers to
primes (Boeing, RTX, Lockheed, Northrop) and the Tier-1s between. The buyer is
the owner, the quality manager, or the operations VP. They are forced to maintain
AS9100 certification and to satisfy each customer's recurring supplier-quality
requirements — and each new or renewing customer makes them resubmit
substantially the same evidence in a slightly different format, which is pure
recurring overhead that nonetheless gates their ability to keep and win purchase
orders.

## The pain (why it's a must-have)
- Primes and Tier-1s CONTRACTUALLY require their suppliers to hold current AS9100
  certification and to maintain recurring supplier-quality evidence (FAI packages,
  counterfeit-parts and conflict-minerals attestations, on-time-delivery and
  escape/quality scorecards, corrective actions) as a condition of staying on the
  Approved Supplier List and receiving POs.
- The pain is ACUTE and revenue-gating: a missed re-qualification, a late SCAR
  response, or a failed FAI gets a supplier flagged, frozen out of new POs, or
  removed from the ASL — a direct, immediate hit to the order book the owner feels.
- It RECURS continuously: AS9100 surveillance audits, every new part's FAI, every
  customer's annual supplier re-survey, and every quality escape generates a fresh
  evidence cycle, forever.
- It is multiplied across customers: a supplier serving a dozen primes/Tier-1s
  rebuilds essentially the same qualification package a dozen times in a dozen
  formats — a continuous, budgeted overhead that scales with their customer count.

## The product
A fixed-scope recurring service on one delivery spine, software-led:
1. Onboarding: build the supplier's structured quality-evidence system-of-record —
   AS9100 documentation, process capability, FAI/AS9102 templates, counterfeit-
   parts and conflict-minerals program, supplier scorecard data — into one
   verified, reusable package.
2. The shared qualification network (the moat): each supplier's verified
   evidence, once assembled, becomes a reusable "qualification passport" that can
   be presented to any prime/Tier-1 that requests it; and because the same
   buyers, the same standards, and the same sub-tier suppliers recur across the
   network, every new supplier and every new buyer requirement is satisfied faster
   from accumulated, verified precedent. The network has genuine two-sided pull:
   buyers increasingly accept the verified package, and suppliers join to reuse
   it — the value compounds with every supplier and every buyer added.
3. Recurring managed cadence: keep the AS9100 evidence and process data current
   for surveillance audits, generate FAI/AS9102 packages per new part, turn around
   each customer's recurring supplier re-survey and SCAR on a fast cadence, and
   keep the counterfeit-parts/conflict-minerals attestations current.
4. Audit/scorecard support: prepare the supplier for AS9100 surveillance and for
   each customer's supplier audit, and keep the customer scorecards from slipping.
The software runs the evidence system-of-record, the FAI/survey templates, the
re-survey/SCAR workflow, and the reusable-package matching; humans handle the
quality judgment and customer-facing responses. The supplier owns and certifies
its own quality data; this service maintains, structures, and re-presents it. No
field operations — the supplier's own staff run the shop floor and the physical
inspections.

## Revenue model
Recurring subscription per supplier, scaled by the number of customer
relationships and part numbers managed (mid four to low five figures per year),
billed annually or monthly in advance, with per-FAI or per-new-buyer-package
overage for high-volume suppliers. Revenue is structurally recurring because
AS9100 surveillance, FAIs, re-surveys, and SCARs never stop, and every new
customer the supplier wins adds a managed relationship and reuses the passport.

## Positioning
"Qualify once, sell to every prime. Stop rebuilding the same AS9100 and FAI
package for every customer — we keep your quality evidence current and reusable,
turn around every supplier survey and corrective action fast, and keep you on
every Approved Supplier List, so you never lose a PO to paperwork." Sold against
the supplier's overworked quality manager doing it in spreadsheets and binders,
generalist AS9100 consultants who bill hourly for a one-time cert and disappear,
and the do-nothing default that risks an ASL removal.

## Go-to-market
Marketing/positioning-led demand generation into aerospace-supplier owners and
quality managers (content on staying on the ASL, FAI traps, SCAR response, and
AS9100 surveillance; targeted outbound to suppliers on prime/Tier-1 supplier
lists; presence in aerospace-manufacturing associations and at supplier-quality
events), plus founder-led close, plus partnerships with the primes' and Tier-1s'
supplier-development teams — who WANT their supply base better-qualified and would
rather accept a trusted reusable package than chase each supplier. Buyer-side
acceptance is what turns the qualification passport into a network.

## Who runs it
Two generalist founders strong in marketing/positioning, software, and operating
formal quality/compliance programs, supported by a named AS9100/aerospace-quality
practitioner (a former supplier-quality engineer or AS9100 auditor) for the
quality judgment. Remote-first, no field operations.
