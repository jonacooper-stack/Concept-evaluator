## KEY TENSION (PM call)
GO (Legal/CTO, scoped to "can it be built/operated legally" — yes) vs REFINE (CFO/CMO/COO, owning demand + delivery). CMO frame is load-bearing — market-pull is the heaviest objectives dimension and CMO says the pull may be intermittent and the channel may not exist. The two GO votes come from the experts whose lanes don't own the demand question.

## TOP 5 RISKS
1. No inbound channel + episodic willingness-to-pay (nobody searches it; the natural distributor — the lender — is conflicted; buyers may want a one-time waiver fix, not a retainer).
2. Unverified customer truth / ARPU (catastrophe-insurance pricing vs bookkeeping pricing; no borrower has been asked; $4-5K ARPU triples the customer count needed).
3. Certificate-correctness / E&O liability tail (small fee, seven-figure downstream harm; cap pierceable for gross negligence).
4. Zero-tolerance QC against scarce judgment labor at the quarter-close peak (~55% Dec-FYE compresses the year into 8 weeks).
5. Onboarding doesn't standardize (~9-hour artisanal loan-agreement abstraction per client throttles growth).

## BIGGEST OPEN QUESTION
Will a lower-middle-market borrower who hasn't recently been burned pay a continuous quarterly subscription (~$1,000+/quarter) for covenant insurance, reachable through a non-conflicted repeatable channel without an inbound engine? Everything else is solved or conditional on this.

## PATH TOWARD GO (60-90 day validation; re-enter clean-room)
1. Prove customer truth + ARPU — 10+ CFO interviews + 5-10 paid pilots at three price points.
2. Commit to and validate one non-inbound channel — signed pilot-referral/white-label with 2-3 fractional-CFO firms; CAC <~$5K.
3. Bundle always-on monitoring with explicit breach-response/waiver-support (converts episodic pain into a subscription; lifts ARPU + retention).
4. Design the operating model around the QC failure mode + quarter-close peak (counsel-drafted LOL + management-certifies wall, E&O from day one, fractional reviewer bench, data SLAs).
5. Prove onboarding standardizes — diff 10 real credit agreements.
