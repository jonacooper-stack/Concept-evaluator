## HOW THE FINALISTS DIFFER (customer / market / revenue mechanism)
- **SOC 1 Readiness** — *enterprise-contract* mandate · niche service orgs (claims processors, payroll/benefits admins, loan servicers) · annual readiness subscription. Distinct: the only contract-driven (not statute/regulator) mandate, with a uniquely clean reach-down moat (Vanta/Drata deliberately skip SOC 1).
- **CoverHold** — *private-insurer* mandate · habitational/small-commercial property owner-managers · monthly per-portfolio. Distinct: hard-market loss-control attestation; the mandate-issuer is a private insurer, sits on the founders' direct insurance edge.
- **CovenantClear** — *lending-bank* mandate · lower-middle-market borrowers with covenant debt · quarterly. Distinct: the only lender-debt-covenant play, pure finance-CFO motion, quarterly cadence.
- **CarrierProof** — *insurance-carrier* mandate · independent insurance agencies/MGAs · annual retainer + monitoring. Distinct: the customer is the distributor *inside* insurance; the lever is losing a carrier appointment and its commission book.
- **LIHTC Compliance** — *IRS Section 42* mandate · affordable-housing owners/managers · per-property annual. Distinct: tax-credit recapture mandate, real-estate customer, per-door pricing.

## THE SINGLE CHEAPEST EXPERIMENT THAT MOST MOVES EACH FINALIST
- **SOC 1 Readiness:** draft control objectives for 3 distinct service-org verticals from real sample report ToCs and measure overlap (≥~60% = a productizable library, escapes the boutique trap; low = labor-bound consultancy). Plus first-3-onboarding hours.
- **CoverHold:** 15 broker/MGA interviews → ≥3 verbal referral commitments (the trigger event is invisible/unscrapeable, so the whole GTM rests on the partner channel) + 5 paid pilots for real ARPU.
- **CovenantClear:** 10+ CFO interviews + 5-10 paid pilots at three price points — is willingness-to-pay continuous or episodic, and at what ARPU?
- **CarrierProof:** of 15-20 agency principals, the % personally burned by a lost/suspended appointment AND willing to commit ≥$6K/yr — painkiller vs vitamin; plus an AgentSync-downmarket moat (cluster/IIABA endorsement).
- **LIHTC:** time-and-motion on 20-25 real certifications (credentialed-review minutes/cert) — the single number that decides scalable product vs throughput-capped staffing shop.

## RECURRING FAILURE MODES (seeds for any next round)
1. **"Reasoned, not evidenced" demand.** Every concept's must-have claim is logical but unvalidated; market-pull (weight 20) is repeatedly the lowest objectives dimension. Future generation should favor niches where the trigger event is *findable/scrapeable* (a public deadline, registry, or filing) rather than a private letter.
2. **Catastrophic-liability coupling.** "Never lose your X" promises sit one causal step from the customer's disaster (lost coverage, called loan, credit recapture, lost appointment), producing a 5 on liability/QC and an E&O tail on a low-five-figure fee. Favor mandates where the service's error is *not* directly causal to a seven-figure loss.
3. **Scarce-specialist bottleneck / no inbound channel.** Judgment-heavy delivery (LIHTC credentialed reviewer, SOC 1 controls specialist, covenant finance reviewer) caps margin and growth; no-search categories force slow relationship GTM. Favor concepts with a deeper labor pool and an enumerable, list-able buyer with a public trigger.
