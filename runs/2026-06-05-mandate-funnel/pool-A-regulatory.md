# Idea Pool A — Existential Regulatory / Licensing / Standards Mandates (63 theses)

Theme: non-compliance is existential — lose a license, accreditation, billing privileges, or the right to operate. Sleepy non-tech incumbents; bridgeable expertise moat via advisor/named practitioner.

1. RadShield Renewals — Diagnostic imaging/dental/vet practices with X-ray/CT | State radiation-machine registration, shielding re-evaluations, physicist surveys due on rolling deadlines; lapse = machine ordered offline | Annual managed-registry subscription per tube/deadline/survey/state | Sleepy local health-physics consultants; contracted licensed physicist for sign-off.
2. PourPermit — Craft breweries/distilleries/wineries | TTB brewer's notices + state ABC renewals, COLA approvals, excise filings; operate unlicensed = shut down/fined | Per-location annual retainer (renewals, COLA, excise calendar) | Old-line beverage-license attorneys/bookkeepers.
