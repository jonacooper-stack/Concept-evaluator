# Idea Pool B — Private-Party Existential Forced-Buys (62 theses)

Theme: failing to comply is existential because a powerful counterparty (insurer, lender, franchisor, prime, payer, marketplace, accreditor, bank-partner) cuts the customer off.

1. **HoldFast** — Restaurant/retail franchisees | Franchisor-mandated brand-standards & operations audits that must pass to keep the franchise; failing repeat audits triggers default/termination notices | Monthly per-location managed audit-prep + evidence-collection subscription with quarterly mock-audit | Sleepy incumbents are franchisor field reps and generic ops consultants.
2. **LienGuard** — Commercial subcontractors on bonded projects | Surety/GC-mandated lien-waiver, COI, compliance-document packages that must be current or progress payments are withheld and the sub is removed | Per-project + monthly retainer managing document chain and renewals | Incumbents are paper-pushing back offices.
3. **PayerPass** — Solo/small allied-health practices (PT, OT, behavioral, SLP) | Health-plan-mandated re-credentialing/CAQH attestation cycles; lapse freezes reimbursement and drops provider from networks | Monthly per-provider managed credentialing-maintenance subscription | Incumbents are sleepy credentialing VARs/billing companies.
4. **CovenantClear** — Lower-middle-market borrowers with bank/SBA term loans | Lender-mandated quarterly covenant compliance certificates/borrowing-base reports; a miss is technical default that can call the loan | Quarterly recurring covenant-reporting managed service | Incumbents are overworked outside CPAs and generic CFO consultants.
5. **NetVerify** — Specialty sellers on B2B/healthcare marketplaces and GPOs | Marketplace/GPO-mandated seller verification/licensure/product-compliance attestations; unmet = delist | Monthly managed verification-maintenance subscription | Incumbents are slow marketplace trust teams and broker middlemen.
6. **CarrierProof** — Independent insurance agencies and MGAs | Carrier-mandated E&O, data-security, producer-licensing attestations to keep appointments; lapse pulls appointment and book | Annual + monitoring recurring retainer | Incumbents are sleepy agency-management consultants; founders' insurance edge direct.
7. **StaffShield** — Staffing agencies supplying healthcare/industrial | Client/MSP-mandated vendor compliance packets (background, drug, immunization, insurance, I-9) or removed from VMS approved-vendor list | Monthly per-agency managed compliance-packet subscription | Incumbents are manual VMS coordinators.
8. **TrialReady** — Small clinical research sites/investigators | Sponsor/CRO-mandated site qualification, training, regulatory-binder attestations to stay on a trial; lapse gets site dropped and unpaid | Per-study + monthly managed regulatory-binder subscription | Incumbents are part-time regulatory coordinators.
9. **DealerComply** — Auto/equipment/RV dealerships | Floorplan-lender (captive finance) mandated inventory audits, insurance, reporting; failed floorplan audit freezes financing | Monthly managed floorplan-compliance + audit-prep subscription | Incumbents are old-line dealer-services consultants.
10. **GrantGuard** — Nonprofits and FQHCs on restricted grants | Funder-mandated compliance reporting, single-audit readiness, sub-recipient monitoring; failure claws back funding and bars future awards | Monthly managed grant-compliance subscription | Incumbents are generalist nonprofit CPAs.

12. **DataPactRx** — Small healthcare SaaS/digital-health vendors | Health-system/payer-mandated security & HIPAA attestations (HITRUST-lite, vendor risk questionnaires) to close/renew enterprise deals | Monthly managed vendor-security-response subscription | Incumbents are hourly boutique HIPAA consultants (NOT the Vanta lane — questionnaire/attestation response).
