# Concept One-Pager

## What it is
A recurring managed service that defends small and mid-size businesses against
being overcharged on their workers'-compensation and general-liability insurance
— continuously managing the carrier's mandatory annual premium audit, correcting
payroll-classification and class-code errors, disputing audit overcharges, and
optimizing the business's experience-modification ("ex-mod") factor — so the
business stops leaking thousands of dollars a year in premium it does not owe.
The service combines software (a class-code and audit system-of-record plus an
accumulating benchmark of audit outcomes) with expert review, and is paid largely
on the money it saves.

## The customer
Privately-owned small and mid-size businesses with meaningful workers'-comp
exposure: construction and specialty trades, manufacturing, staffing agencies,
trucking and logistics, landscaping, restaurants/hospitality groups, home-health
and senior-care operators — typically 25–500 employees with annual workers'-comp
premiums of roughly $75K–$2M. The buyer is the owner, CFO, or controller. They
already pay the premium (it is one of their largest line items), they are
subjected to a carrier premium audit every single policy year, and they have no
in-house insurance expertise to know whether the audit bill is correct — so they
pay whatever the carrier's auditor says.

## The pain (why it's a must-have)
- Every workers'-comp and auditable GL policy carries a CARRIER-MANDATED annual
  premium audit; the audit reconciles estimated vs actual payroll by class code
  and routinely produces additional-premium bills.
- Misclassification of employees into higher-rated class codes, payroll not
  properly split, overstated remuneration, and ex-mod errors systematically
  OVERCHARGE employers — and the carrier's auditor has no incentive to catch
  errors in the employer's favor.
- The overcharge recurs every policy year and compounds (a wrong class code or a
  data error in the ex-mod calculation follows the business for three years), so
  the leak is continuous, not one-time.
- It is real money against a real budget line: a 5–15% premium error on a $300K
  workers'-comp program is $15K–$45K a year, every year, and the business has no
  way to know unless someone who reads class-code rules looks.

## The product
A fixed delivery spine combining software and expert review:
1. Onboarding: ingest the business's policies, loss runs, payroll records, prior
   audits, and ex-mod worksheets; build the class-code and exposure
   system-of-record; identify historical overcharges that can be recovered.
2. A continuous portal holding the class-code map, payroll-split rules, audit
   calendar, dispute history, and ex-mod tracking — plus an accumulating,
   anonymized benchmark of audit outcomes and class-code disputes across all
   clients that makes each new dispute stronger.
3. Recurring managed cadence: prepare the business for each annual premium audit
   (organize payroll splits, overtime exclusions, subcontractor documentation),
   sit the audit on the employer's behalf, review the auditor's findings line by
   line, file disputes/revisions on overcharges, and monitor the ex-mod
   calculation for errors every year.
4. Recovery work: pursue refunds on prior-year overcharges and corrected ex-mods.
The software does the class-code logic, audit-calendar tracking, payroll-split
calculations, document assembly, and benchmark comparisons; the expert reviewer
applies judgment on classification disputes. No claims handling, no policy
brokering, no field work — the business keeps its own broker and carrier; this
service sits alongside as the premium-cost watchdog.

## Revenue model
A blend designed for high willingness-to-pay: an annual retainer/subscription per
business (mid four to low five figures, scaled by premium size), PLUS a
contingency take-rate (a share, e.g. 20–30%) of overcharges recovered and
documented first-year savings. Billed so the subscription recurs every policy
year and the contingency captures the upside. Revenue is structurally recurring
because the audit happens every year and the ex-mod must be watched every year;
the take-rate makes the ROI self-evident and the sale easy.

## Positioning
"Stop overpaying your work-comp. Your carrier audits you every year and the
errors only ever go their way — we put an expert and the data on your side,
manage every audit, and win back the premium you never owed. We mostly get paid
from what we save you." Sold against sleepy, reactive premium-audit-dispute
consultants who take a one-off case after the bill arrives, and against brokers
who sell the policy but do not police the audit.

## Go-to-market
Marketing/positioning-led demand generation into owners/CFOs of premium-heavy
businesses (content on premium-audit traps, class-code misclassification, and
ex-mod errors; targeted outbound to high-work-comp-exposure industries;
partnerships with brokers who want a retention tool and with industry
associations and PEOs), plus founder-led close leveraging the founders'
regulated-insurance-sales background. The contingency offer ("we get paid from
what we save you") makes the first meeting easy and referral-rich.

## Who runs it
Two generalist founders with deep experience selling into and operating within
regulated insurance, strong in marketing/positioning and software, supported by a
named workers'-comp premium-audit / class-code expert (a former carrier premium
auditor or workers'-comp consultant) for classification judgment. Remote-first,
no field operations.
