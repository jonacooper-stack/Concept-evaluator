# Concept One-Pager

## What it is
A recurring managed service plus rules-engine that captures the utility and
government energy-efficiency rebates and incentives that HVAC, electrical,
plumbing, solar, and building-envelope contractors are entitled to on the work
they already do — identifying the eligible programs for each job, preparing and
filing the rebate/incentive paperwork correctly and on deadline, tracking the
claim to payment, and reconciling what gets paid — so the contractor stops
leaving money on the table and can reliably promise the rebate to the customer at
the point of sale. The product is anchored by a continuously-maintained,
proprietary database of utility/state/federal program rules, amounts, eligibility,
and filing requirements across thousands of jurisdictions and programs.

## The customer
Privately-owned residential and light-commercial trade contractors whose work
qualifies for efficiency rebates: HVAC and heat-pump installers, electrical and
electrification contractors, solar and storage installers, insulation/weatherization
and window/door firms, water-heating and plumbing contractors — typically $1M–$30M
in revenue doing dozens to hundreds of qualifying jobs a month. The buyer is the
owner or the operations/sales manager. Rebates are increasingly central to closing
the sale (the customer expects the contractor to "handle the rebate"), and the
rebate dollars are real margin on jobs already sold — but the programs change
constantly, the paperwork is fiddly and deadline-bound, and contractors routinely
miss, mis-file, or never claim them.

## The pain (why it's a must-have)
- Rebates and incentives are now woven into the unit economics and the SALES
  PITCH of these trades: with federal (e.g., Inflation Reduction Act
  electrification incentives, 25C/179D), state, and utility programs stacking,
  the customer expects the rebate and the contractor's price/close depends on it.
  A contractor who can't reliably deliver the rebate loses jobs to one who can.
- The money is already earned on completed work; a missed or rejected filing is
  pure lost margin (often hundreds to low thousands of dollars per job) the owner
  feels directly.
- Programs change funding levels, rules, and deadlines constantly and vary by
  utility and jurisdiction; keeping current is a real, continuous research burden
  no contractor has staff for.
- The work recurs with every job, forever — it is intrinsically high-frequency,
  not a one-time setup.

## The product
A fixed-scope recurring service on one delivery spine, software-led:
1. Onboarding: map the contractor's service area, trades, and typical jobs to
   every applicable rebate/incentive program; integrate a lightweight job intake.
2. The proprietary rules engine + program database: the live, structured source
   of truth on every utility/state/federal program — amounts, eligibility,
   documentation, portals, and deadlines — that the founders maintain and that
   compounds as more programs and more filed outcomes are captured (a benchmark
   of what actually gets approved).
3. Recurring managed cadence: for each qualifying job, auto-identify the best
   eligible program(s), assemble and file the rebate/incentive application with
   the required documentation, track it to payment, and chase rejections — at
   high volume, with software doing the matching/forms and humans handling
   exceptions.
4. Reporting: a per-job and portfolio view of rebate dollars captured, pending,
   and at-risk, plus point-of-sale tools so the contractor's salespeople can
   quote the rebate confidently.

## Revenue model
A blend built for self-evident ROI: a recurring software subscription for the
rules engine and point-of-sale rebate tools (mid four to low five figures per
year, scaled by job volume), PLUS a take-rate (a percentage, e.g. 10–20%) of the
rebate/incentive dollars actually captured and paid. Billed monthly; the
subscription recurs and the take-rate scales with the contractor's volume.
Revenue is structurally recurring and grows with each contractor's business.

## Positioning
"Capture every rebate, win every job. Your customers expect the rebate and the
programs change every quarter — we find every incentive your job qualifies for,
file it right, and chase it to payment, so you quote with confidence and never
leave money on the table. We're paid from the rebates we capture." Sold against
the contractor's overworked office staff filing rebates by hand, one-off rebate-
processing clerks, and the do-nothing default where the contractor simply eats
the lost incentive.

## Go-to-market
Marketing/positioning-led demand generation into trade contractors (content on
new IRA/state/utility programs and deadlines, "rebates you're missing"
calculators; targeted outbound to HVAC/solar/electrification firms; partnerships
with equipment distributors and manufacturer rep firms who want their dealers to
sell more efficient equipment, and with trade associations), plus founder-led
close. Distributors and manufacturers are strong referral channels because more
captured rebates means more equipment sold.

## Who runs it
Two generalist founders strong in marketing/positioning, software, and operating
data-heavy programs, supported by a named energy-incentive / rebate-program
specialist for program-rule judgment. Remote-first, no field operations — the
contractor and its crews do all the physical installation work.
